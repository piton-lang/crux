Shape, space and feel

Spacing
8px unit. 16px between tiles and cards, 22 to 40px padding inside blocks, 40px between sections.
Density
Airy. Big blocks, big numbers, about four project cards per screen.
Corners
20 to 48px. Blocks get one tighter 10px corner so they wobble like blobs. Buttons, fields and pills are fully round.
Borders
None on blocks. Fields carry a 3px warm #eadfce border.
Shadows
No blur. A flat 6 to 8px ledge straight down, #f1e8da under white cards and #4b2aa3 under the grape button.
Icons
Line icons at 1.25em with a fat 2.4 round stroke. Status uses filled shapes, a ticked circle, a rounded triangle and a rounded square with a dash.
Tone
Warm and encouraging, sentence case. “Nice work this week!”, “need a little love”, “See them all”.
