Spacing, shape and tone

Layout
A device body up to 396px, centred on the bench, with four corner screws. An engraved header plate holds the brand, a green power LED and the avatar, over an inset readout with the date and big task and meeting counts. Below sit two graph-paper panels, Scheduled runs with a time-axis plot and two run rows, then Samples with six sample labels. A control strip of round knobs and a square green add key ends the device.
Spacing
4px base, 8px grid squares. 12px between panels, 8px between samples, 6 to 10px padding.
Density
Dense but ordered. Every value carries a unit or a delta.
Corners
2 to 6px, slightly softened metal. Knobs and screws are round.
Borders
1px bezel lines everywhere. Sample labels have a 6px coloured tab on the left, hatched for overdue. The current knob gets a 2px ink ring and a small triangle pointer.
Shadows
Inset 0 1px 3px shadows for readouts and fields, a white top highlight and 2px hard bottom edge for the chassis and keys.
Icons
1.8 stroke line icons inside knob discs, such as a flask for Projects. Status flags pair a word with a filled dot, hollow ring, tick or cross.
Tone
Measured and technical. Runs, Samples, Queued, Δt 30 min, +2 d.
