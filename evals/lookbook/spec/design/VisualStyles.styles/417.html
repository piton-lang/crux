Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
8px unit. 28 to 32px page padding, 20px between stamp rows, 32px between spreads.
Density
Medium. Two spreads of two pages, six stamps in a three column grid.
Corners
Square pages with a soft spine. Circle, oval and 10px square stamps, 2px fields and buttons.
Borders
Double borders on inked stamps, 2px dashed borders on blanks, dotted rules in the checklist.
Shadows
A spine shadow inside each page where the spread folds, and a deep drop under each spread.
Icons
Small line icons inside stamps. Booked carries a check under a rule, Not booked a dashed circle in a dashed blank, Done a ticked box, To do a dashed empty box.
Tone
Official but kind. Visas and entries, Conditions of travel, Endorsement.
