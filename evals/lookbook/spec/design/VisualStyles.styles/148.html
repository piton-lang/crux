Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
An 8px unit. 32px gutters, 48px between sections, 14 by 20px rows.
Density
Airy and festive. Large flags and generous rows.
Corners
Pills for buttons, tabs, fields and status. 18px panels. Flags have zigzag bottoms and the cards inside them have round arched tops.
Borders
2px ink outlines on panels, buttons and fields, dotted dividers between rows. Every panel starts with a pierced coloured paper strip.
Shadows
Only a hard 4px ink shadow under the primary button.
Icons
Bold rounded line icons. Status uses a ticked circle for On track, a triangle with an exclamation for At risk and a rounded square with a down arrow for Behind, inside outlined pills with the word.
Tone
Cheerful and short, such as Good morning, Mara! and Last week 59.
