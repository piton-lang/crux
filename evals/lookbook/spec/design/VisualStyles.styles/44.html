Build

Spacing
8px unit. 16px between plates, 24px plate padding, 14px row padding.
Density
Medium. One control per row, rows split by a groove line.
Corners
6px plates, 4px keys and rockers, 3px fields and readouts.
Borders
1px #8a9198 on everything metal; grooves are a dark line over a light line.
Shadows
Raised parts have a 3px solid bottom shadow; pressed parts sink 2px with an inset shadow.
Icons
Lamps by shape: round lit lamp means ON, hollow square means OFF, amber triangle marks the selected key. Hazard triangle on danger.
Tone
Terse panel labels in capitals: Operator, Commit, Changes saved.
