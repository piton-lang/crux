Style 344 · Chat slate

A professional three pane chat in blue slate, where a quiet sidebar, a roomy message list and a docked thread panel share one mint accent that only marks where you are and what you can do.

Spacing, density and depth

Spacing
8px unit. 8px 12px message padding, 16px panel padding, 20px gutters.
Density
Comfortable. Five messages, a composer and a thread fit one laptop screen.
Corners
8px avatars, messages and cards, 6px buttons and nav rows, round chips.
Borders
1px #3a4558 between panes and around cards. Panes step in lightness.
Shadows
None. The current channel has an inset 3px mint bar instead.
Icons
Rounded line icons at 1.8 stroke. Presence pairs a dot, crescent or ring with a word.
Focus
2px mint outline offset 2px, around the whole composer when its field has focus.
Tone
Plain workplace labels such as Channels, Direct messages, Thread and 3 replies.
