Style sheet

Style 79 · Tabloid

The dashboard shouts like a tabloid front page: a red masthead, a giant condensed splash for the overdue count, starburst stickers, and every project in a tilted cut-out photo box.

Palette

  • Paper#fbfaf7 · page and photo frames
  • Ink#0b0b0b · type, 3px borders, hard shadows
  • Masthead red#c8102e · masthead, primary button, this week, Behind
  • Highlighter#ffe100 · fact boxes, bursts, At risk, ink text only
  • Grey#4a4a4a · captions
  • Halftone#d9d6cf · dot screen in photo boxes
  • Cut-out grey#9a968e · silhouettes, decoration only
  • Green#0f6b33 · On track sticker
  • Focus blue#123e9c · 4px focus outline

Spacing, corners and depth

Spacing
8px unit, loud and packed. 16 to 22px between boxes, 6px rules between blocks.
Density
Medium. Few words, each one huge.
Corners
Square boxes; only the On track sticker is a pill. Boxes tilt by 1 to 2 degrees.
Borders
3px ink on boxes and fields, 4 to 6px ink rules above and below the splash.
Shadows
Hard offset ink shadows, 4 to 8px, no blur; the chart casts a red one.
Icons
Stickers with different shapes. A green disc with a tick for On track, a yellow sign with a black triangle for At risk, a red jagged burst with a cross for Behind. Starbursts for big numbers.
Tone
Exclaiming headlines: Morning, Mara!, 4 tasks overdue!, Shock as, The big six, Team gossip.
