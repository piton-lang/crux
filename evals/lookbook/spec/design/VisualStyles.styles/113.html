Style 113 · Corkboard

The dashboard is a framed corkboard where index cards, sticky notes and paper slips are held up with push pins, and the project cards hang from a sagging red yarn strung pin to pin.

Palette

  • Wall#efe9dfAround the board, header
  • Cork#d9b98aThe board, speckled with #b8935e and #e8cfa6
  • Frame wood#a87d4cThe 16px board frame
  • Index card#fffdf6Cards, slips, fields, this sheet
  • Ruled blue#a9c4e0Card lines, decoration only
  • Margin red#e08a86The card header line, decoration only
  • Sticky yellow#fff1a8Overdue note, current nav tab
  • Masking tape#efe3c2Section labels
  • Red yarn#b3262eString between pins, this week bars
  • Ink#2b2118Text and marker lines
  • Blue biro#1d5fa8Progress hatching, focus ring
  • Red pen#a8201aPrimary button, overdue, Behind
  • Green pen#236b3aOn track
  • Amber pen#8a4f00At risk

System

Spacing
An 8px unit with 24px card ruling. 40px gaps between cards to leave room for pins and yarn.
Density
Medium. Cards three across, slips stacked in a column.
Corners
Square paper. Controls 6px, marker bars slightly uneven.
Borders
A 16px wooden frame around the cork, 1.5 to 2px ink lines on controls and marker bars.
Shadows
Soft drops under every paper, a hard pin shadow, and an inset shadow inside the frame.
Icons
Solid filled shapes with white marks. Ticked circle for On track, triangle for At risk, crossed square for Behind, a burst for overdue. Push pins in red, blue, green and yellow are decoration.
Tone
Office board notes, such as Pinned up for the team and Latest notes.
