Space, shape and tone

Spacing
8px unit. 48 by 56px inside the blotter, 14px per correspondence line, 32px page gutter.
Density
Low. One elegant list, one letter, a column of tabs.
Corners
Square, with triangular wood blotter corners. Index tabs round only their outer corners 10px.
Borders
1px gold hairlines between lines and around buttons. The blotter has two inset gold tooling lines.
Shadows
One deep shadow under the blotter, nothing else.
Icons
Fine 1.4 stroke. A pen nib plus Unread, a filled star plus Starred, a clip plus Enclosure, § before every label. Italic initials in gold roundels.
Focus
2px gold outline offset 3px with a second fine gold ring outside it.

Formal and courteous. Correspondence, Folios, Enclosure, The desk of Mara Okafor.
