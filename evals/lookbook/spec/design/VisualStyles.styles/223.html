Style 223 · High contrast professional

Strict black on white with heavy 2px rules, large plain type and no colour at all except a bright yellow focus ring, built first for people who need maximum contrast and still calm enough for daily work.

Spacing, density and surfaces

Spacing
An 8px unit. 44px minimum targets, 14px row padding, 40 to 48px between sections.
Density
Medium low. One column for the summary and the table, then chart and activity side by side.
Corners
Square everywhere. Only the avatar and the notification count are round.
Borders
2px black on every box, rule and row. 4px under section titles, 12px on the overdue note's left edge, 8px over the style sheet.
Shadows
None. The only box shadow is the yellow focus ring.
Icons
Line icons at 2px stroke sized at 1.25em. Status shapes are 20px, outlined circle check, outlined triangle, and a solid square in an inverted chip for Behind.
Focus
3px black outline with a 7px yellow ring outside it on white, a 3px yellow outline on black.
