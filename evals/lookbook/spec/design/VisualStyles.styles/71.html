Spacing, shape and tone

Layout
A framed map sheet up to 400px wide, centred on the land colour. A title cartouche with a double frame holds the brand, date, counts and avatar, then a scale bar and compass. Meetings sit as two waypoint cards side by side. Tasks run down a numbered route with a marker column on the left joined by a dashed red line. A legend box closes the map, then the bottom bar.
Spacing
8px unit. 8px between stops, 10px between waypoints, 24px before region labels.
Density
Medium. Each stop is two short lines.
Corners
Square frames and cards. Round avatar, badge, add button and pill buttons.
Borders
1px boundary lines on cards. The cartouche and map have a double frame with a 3 to 4px gap. Overdue cards get a 2px route red border.
Shadows
None. Depth comes from frames and the contour paper.
Icons
Map markers carry status by shape, a teardrop pin, a hollow ring, a flag and a triangle. Meetings use diamonds. Nav icons are thin 1.6 stroke map symbols like a compass and a folded map.
Tone
Gentle surveyor words for headings, Waypoints, The route, Legend. Item text stays plain.
