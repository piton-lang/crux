Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
An 8px unit. 32px gutters between courtyard areas, 16px between niches, 14 to 16px cell padding.
Density
Medium. A full table of projects beside a calm arched greeting.
Corners
Arched. Niches, tabs, buttons, fields and avatars have round tops and 4px bottoms. The greeting is a full horseshoe arch. Panels are 6px.
Borders
2px cobalt frames, zellige bands as dado, footer and the style sheet border, dashed grout lines between feed items.
Shadows
None, except a 4px inner shade at the bottom of the primary button like a glazed tile edge.
Icons
Eight-point stars as markers and the brand. Line icons in cobalt and emerald. Status uses an eight-point star for On track, a triangle with a bar for At risk and a hexagon with a dash for Behind, always with the word.
Tone
Warm and plain, such as Good morning and Green tiles last week.
