Style 52 · Sports magazine

Playbook

Spacing
8px unit. 20px card padding, 24px gutters, 48px between sections.
Density
Busy and punchy. A ticker, stat lists and a packed scoreboard.
Corners
No round corners. Tabs, buttons, badges and the current nav are parallelograms cut at 12px.
Borders
Thick top bars, 8px ink on plans, 6px ink under section heads, 2px stat dividers, a 6px slate frame on the scoreboard.
Shadows
None. Energy comes from stripes and slants.
Icons
SVG jerseys carry the price as the shirt number. Play triangles as bullets, ✔ and ✕ on the scoreboard, a star on the badge, a » on every button.
Focus
3px ink outline offset 2px with a 7px volt glow. Slanted buttons drop their clip while focused.
Tone
Coach talk in caps, such as The roster, The scoreboard and Post-game Q&A.
