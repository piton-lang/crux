Spacing, density and depth

Fonts
One rounded stack, ui-rounded, SF Pro Rounded, Nunito, Varela Round, Arial Rounded MT Bold. Heavy weights, 700 and 800.
Spacing
8px unit. 12px between cards, 16 to 20px card padding, 24px between list and details.
Density
Roomy and chunky. Big 56px blobs and 17px names.
Corners
Very round. 24px cards, 28 to 32px bars and panels, pills for every control, a wobbly jelly frame.
Borders
Mostly none. 3px bubblegum on the selected card, 3px chew rings on fields.
Shadows
Solid 4px ledges under buttons and tabs, inset highlights on blobs.
Icons
2.2px rounded line icons in dark plum on candy blobs. Status is a candy pill with dark text and a star, people, arrow or checked box.
Tone
Friendly and short, Drive, Details, Upload.
Motifs
Sprinkles scattered on the tray, candy striped progress and storage bars.
