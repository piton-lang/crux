Spacing, density, corners and depth

Spacing
An 8px unit, 24 to 28px gaps, roomy 72 to 96px section padding
Density
Airy and toy-like. Short lines, big rounded shapes, one idea per capsule
Corners
Everything bubbly. Pills at 999px, cards 36px, the device bezel 40px, the quote 48px with a tail
Borders
1px silver #8f97b3 around chrome, 3px iridescent gradient rings around cards, 1px white highlights on top edges
Shadows
Soft drops of 0 16px 40px rgba(28,27,77,0.12), plus inner shading at the bottom and a white gloss strip on the top half of every pill and card
Ornament
Four-point star sparkles in magenta and periwinkle, glossy glass orbs, faint pink, blue and mint glows on the page
Icons
Thick round 2.4 stroke white glyphs inside glossy 84px spheres. Status gems are a round tick, a diamond with ! and a rounded square with ×, beside the word
Focus
A white 3px ring, then a 3px magenta ring and a soft magenta glow
Tone
Upbeat and bouncy, such as Everything bubbles up to one page and My projects
