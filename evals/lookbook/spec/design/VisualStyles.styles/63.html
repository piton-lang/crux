Form and finish

Spacing
8px unit. 24px between cards, 40px gutter around the centre stripe, 18 to 20px card padding.
Density
Loose and loud. Two staggered columns of day cards, each event on its own white pill card.
Corners
Big and lopsided. Day cards alternate 48px and 16px corners, events are 20px, controls are full pills.
Borders
Thick plum outlines, 3px on cards and controls. Deadline borders are dashed, out of office dotted.
Shadows
Hard offsets only, 4px plum under the primary button and 6px magenta under the today card.
Icons
Chunky filled shapes in 30px round badges. Four petal flower for Team, lightning bolt for Deadline, rainbow arcs for Out of office.
Focus
4px plum outline offset 3px with a 10px sunshine halo.
Tone
Playful and warm: Groovy week ahead, Tune in, Far out, Free and easy.
