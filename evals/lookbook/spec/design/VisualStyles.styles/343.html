Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
4px unit. 4px between rows, 2px seams between cells, 16px board padding, 24px gutter, 16px on phones.
Density
High and even. Seven deal rows read like one board, every cell one terse value.
Corners
2px. The board is square.
Borders
1px frames on boards, 2px black gaps between cells, a 3px red underline on the stalled row.
Shadows
None. The tile seam, a 2 to 3 percent black band, gives the flap depth.
Icons
Solid signal lamps lead every row. Hollow circle open, diamond booked, square sent, triangle stalled, play final call, check landed, each with its word.
Focus
A 3px amber outline offset 2px.
Tone
Departure board shorthand. Booked, Final call, Landed, No reply 12 days, Notices.
