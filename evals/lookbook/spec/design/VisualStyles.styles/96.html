Spacing, density and finish

Spacing
8px unit. Pages have 40px inner margins and 32px between sections. Everything is centred on the page axis.
Density
Medium. Leader dots let names and values share long lines, as in a cast list.
Corners
Square pages. The valance hangs in soft scallops.
Borders
Each page has a 1px ink frame with a 3px brass rule inside it and a gap between. Ornamental rules with a looped centre divide sections.
Shadows
Only the spine, a soft inner shadow where the two pages meet.
Icons
Small solid emblems. A four pointed spark for On track, a half moon for At risk, a falling curtain triangle for Behind and overdue.
Tone
Front of house voice, such as Tonight's figures, The company and Enter Sam Ortiz.
