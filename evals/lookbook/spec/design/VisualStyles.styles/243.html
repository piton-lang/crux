Spacing, density and surfaces

Spacing
An 8px unit. 32px between entries, 72px between sections, 24px column gutter on small screens.
Density
Sparse. One column, long line height and wide gaps.
Corners
Round pill buttons and round flames, otherwise no boxes at all.
Borders
Hairlines only, 1px at rest between entries and under the search field.
Shadows
No box shadows. Warmth comes from radial candle glows at 9 to 12 percent and a soft blur round each flame.
Icons
Thin line icons at 1.5px stroke. Status uses a check circle, a triangle with a bang and a crossed square, always with the word, overdue a clock.
Focus
A 2px flame outline offset 4px with a soft flame glow.
Tone
Intimate and gentle, such as Good morning, Mara, A quiet note on the week and From the journal.
