Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
8px unit. 44px around the title, 32px between the key wall and the register, 6px wooden frame between boxes.
Density
Medium. Six key boxes, one register, a telegram and a small framed map.
Corners
Square throughout. Only the brass number plates round by 2px.
Borders
A thick dark wood frame round every box, double brass rules in the marquee, dotted leaders in the checks, a brass bar on the telegram.
Shadows
Deep inset shade at the top of each box, a long drop under the key wall and the register, diagonal light bands from blinds.
Icons
Drawn objects tell state. Booked is a brass key on its hook, Not booked an empty hook over a dashed square, a flight is a white letter. Done is a check, To do a dash.
Tone
Discreet concierge. The front desk presents, Key boxes, Vacant, Check out.
