Style 38 · Comic book

The board as a comic page, with inked panels on white gutters, halftone dot skies, task cards spoken in speech bubbles and a starburst that shouts what is overdue.

Spacing, corners and depth

Spacing
12px white gutters between every panel · 26px between bubbles to leave room for tails and speakers
Density
Medium. One bubble and one speaker per task.
Corners
Square panels and caption boxes · 26px speech bubbles · 20px fields · round faces
Borders
4px ink on panels and buttons, 3px on bubbles, captions and fields. The overdue bubble turns 4px red.
Shadows
None on boxes. Only the title lettering gets a solid 4px ink drop.
Icons
Heavy 2.6 stroke glyphs. Overdue is a starburst with a triangle and the word Overdue. Done is an ink tag with a tick. Sound words such as POW are decoration only.
Tone
Comic narration in captions such as Meanwhile, on the board. Task text stays plain, in caps lettering.
