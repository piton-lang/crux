Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
An 8px unit. 32px text margins, 40px between columns, 14px inside each register entry.
Density
Medium. A full text block per column, read slowly like a page.
Corners
Square, except the folio's worn top right corner and arched swatches and chart bars.
Borders
Red double rules under the header, double ruling between margin and text, hairline ruling between entries.
Shadows
None. Frames are layered rings of gold, vellum and lapis.
Icons
Hand-drawn line icons at text size, pilcrows, a fleuron and a manicule-free plain bell. Status uses a quatrefoil for On track, a triangle for At risk and a crossed square for Behind, always with the word.
Tone
Gently archaic, such as Good morrow, Take heed and The register of works, never at the cost of clarity.
