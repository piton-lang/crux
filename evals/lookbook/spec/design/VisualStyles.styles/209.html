Style 209 · Scorecard

A board report you can work from: every figure sits on a ruled row beside its target, drawn as a bullet chart with a red target tick, so the page answers whether the team is meeting its numbers.

Palette

  • Page#f6f6f3
    The page behind the report
  • Paper#ffffff
    Top bar, tables and the style sheet
  • Ink navy#14213d
    Text, actual bars, primary button, heavy rules
  • Sub#4a5468
    Notes, column heads and dates
  • Rule#d5d6d0
    Row rules and field borders, never text
  • Band poor#c5cad3
    Bullet poor range and last week bars
  • Band fair#dadde3
    Bullet fair range and initials
  • Band good#eceef1
    Bullet good range
  • Target red#b42318
    Target ticks, Missed and the overdue line
  • Met green#166534
    Met result and positive variance
  • Near amber#8a4b00
    Near result
  • Focus blue#1d4ed8
    The 2px focus outline

System

Spacing
8px unit. Rows 14px tall padding, sections 40px apart, page gutter 24px, 16px on phones.
Density
Medium. One measure per row with its actual, target, chart, variance and result side by side.
Corners
Square everywhere, including buttons, fields and status marks.
Borders
1px warm grey rules between rows. A 2px navy rule under the top bar and under each section label.
Shadows
None.
Icons
Thin square-capped line icons at text size. Results use filled shapes, a circle for Met, a triangle for Near and a square for Missed, always with the word.
Tone
Plain and numerate, like a finance pack. Labels name the measure, figures carry the meaning.
