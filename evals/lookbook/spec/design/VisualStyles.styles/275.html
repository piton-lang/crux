Spacing, density and depth

Fonts
Titles in a high contrast serif, Bodoni 72, Didot, Big Caslon, Times New Roman. Everything else in Courier New typewriter.
Spacing
8px unit. 40px gutters, 12px cell padding, 24px blocks.
Density
Medium. A numbered ledger with dotted rules.
Corners
Square. Folder tabs are trapezoids.
Borders
Double rules above heads and sections, dotted rules between rows, 1px grey outlines on secondary controls.
Shadows
Only a hard black offset under the tilted print.
Icons
Thin 1.4px line icons in grey and white. Status is a bracketed typed word with a star, people or arrow.
Tone
Typed and terse, case file, Contents, Notes.
Motifs
Diagonal blind shadows across the header wall, the tilted photo print, the reversed selected row.
