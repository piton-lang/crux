Style 240 · Casino felt

Spacing, density and surfaces

Spacing
An 8px unit. 40px table padding, 16px card padding, 24px between chips.
Density
Medium low. The table is roomy and the cards are small but few.
Corners
56px rounded table ends, 10px cards, round chips, 6px buttons and fields.
Borders
A 14px leather rail, a 1px gold line inset in the felt, 1px gold card borders with a second inner gold line.
Shadows
Cards cast 0 10px 20px of black at 45 percent onto the felt, chips cast 0 4px 0 of a darker rim, and the felt has an inner vignette.
Icons
Line icons at 1.8px stroke. Status uses a check circle, a triangle with a bang and a crossed square, shown as the card index symbol and with the word, overdue a clock.
Focus
A 2px gold outline offset 3px.
Tone
Light and a little playful, such as The hand, Chips this week and Dealer's log, never gambling talk.
