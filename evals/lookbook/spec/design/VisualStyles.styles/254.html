Spacing, density and depth

Spacing
8px unit. 16px gutters around trays, 18px panel padding, 10px between fields.
Density
Medium. Seven layer rows with a sub line each, five inspector tiers.
Corners
10px trays, 6px tiles, fields and buttons, round seal and avatars.
Borders
1px gold line rims on every tray, 1px seam borders on fields, a 3px gold edge on the selected layer.
Shadows
Deep soft shadow under trays and the frame, 0 12px 28px black at 55 percent, plus a faint warm top gloss.
Icons
18px line icons at 1.5 stroke. Selected uses a circled check, Collapsed a right chevron, Saved a cloud with a check.
Focus
2px gold outline offset 3px.
Tone
Quiet and formal. Caps tier names such as Position and size, Export.
