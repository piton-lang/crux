Spacing, density and depth

Spacing
8px unit. 28px between messages, 64px between index and log, 56px top margin.
Density
Sparse. A 62 character measure and generous leading, like reading prose.
Corners
None anywhere. There are no shapes to round.
Borders
Only rules. A 1px muted left rule marks the thread indent and underlines the composer.
Shadows
None.
Icons
No icons. Words and typographic marks, an arrow for the current channel and ● ◐ ○ for presence.
Focus
A 2px citron underline with a faint warm wash behind the control.
Tone
Quiet and literary, such as shared an image, 3 replies in thread and Priya is typing….
