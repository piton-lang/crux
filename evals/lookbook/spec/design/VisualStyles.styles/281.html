Spacing, density and depth

Fonts
One stack, Trebuchet MS, Segoe UI, Tahoma. Lettering is italic 900 capitals with a hard black drop.
Spacing
8px unit. 14px gutters, 16 to 18px panel padding, 8px between file frames.
Density
Medium. Each file is its own small frame.
Corners
Square panels with slanted edges cut by clip paths, round speech bubbles with tails, pill search.
Borders
3px cream ink around panels, 2px warm grey around file frames, 3px red around the selected frame.
Shadows
Hard black offsets on captions, buttons, lettering and the selected frame.
Icons
2px line icons in cream. Status is a speech bubble with a word and a star, people, arrow or checked box. Starred is the red bubble.
Tone
Comic captions, short and punchy, 8 files, brand-guide-v3.pdf is selected.
Motifs
Halftone dots in the title panel, the storage bar and the preview, a light shaft across the preview.
