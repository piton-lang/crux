Spacing, corners and depth

Spacing
Set by the 26px ruled line. 22px between cards, 18px card padding.
Density
Medium. Four facts per card plus a jug.
Corners
3px card corners; buttons and fields use uneven hand-cut corners such as 4px 16px.
Borders
Gingham frame round the page, a 2px red title rule and blue lines on cards, 2px pencil outlines on controls.
Shadows
Soft paper shadow under cards, a 2px by 3px pencil offset under buttons. Cards tilt under one degree.
Icons
Kitchen drawings: jug, spoon, timer, sand glass. Stamps pair the word with a ticked circle for On track, a triangle for At risk and a crossed square for Behind.
Tone
Warm and homely: Heads up, check the oven first, Recipes on the go, Kitchen notes, Ready by.
