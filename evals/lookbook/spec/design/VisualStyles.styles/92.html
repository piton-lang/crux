spacing, shape and voice

Spacing
8px unit. 40px sleeve padding, 14px per track, 40px between sides.
Density
Medium. One huge cover, then a compact back with six tracks.
Corners
Square sleeves. Everything you press is a full pill, and the record, label, swatches and avatar are circles.
Borders
2px ink on controls, 3px ink rules on the sleeve back, dashed groove lines between tracks.
Shadows
The sleeve lifts with a long soft 40px shadow. No other shadows.
Icons
Solid simple shapes. On track a disc, At risk a triangle, Behind a square and overdue a starburst like a hype sticker.
Tone
Record shop chatter, lowercase headings. side a, liner notes, Released Saturday, tracks A1 to B3.
