Style 167 · Dot-matrix greenbar

The dashboard is one long dot-matrix printout on continuous greenbar paper: tractor-feed margins, banded rows every three lines, a run header, fixed-width columns and charts drawn with asterisks.

Spacing, density and depth

Spacing
A 20px line grid. Everything sits on whole lines, bands are 60px, gaps are one blank line.
Density
Very dense, report style. Fixed-width columns and full caps.
Corners
None. Only the tractor holes are round.
Borders
Typed rules of equals and hyphen characters, 2px dashed lines for column heads and summary boxes, dashed perforations.
Shadows
A faint 1px shadow under the paper. Type gets a 0.4px side shadow for impact weight.
Icons
ASCII only. (o) On track, /!\ At risk, [X] Behind, <!> Overdue, asterisks for bars, hashes for progress.
Tone
Batch-job report, such as RUN DATE, QUERY> and *** END OF REPORT ***.
