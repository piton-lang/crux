Rules

Spacing
8px unit. 16px gaps between tiles and inside them, 24px between bands.
Density
Medium high. Six instruments at once, each with a chart and three numbers.
Corners
12px tiles and panels, 10px controls and rail items, 6px chips, 3px bars.
Borders
1px cool grey on every tile, with ruled stat footers and a 4px progress edge at the base.
Shadows
One soft pair, 0 1px 2px and 0 2px 8px at 5 to 6 percent ink.
Icons
1.8px round line icons with labels in the rail. Status is a solid check disc, diamond or down triangle in a tinted chip with its word.
Tone
Instrument labels, such as Complete, Tasks, Due 14 Nov and 7 done this week.
