Style 103 · Dominoes

Spacing, density and finish

Spacing
8px unit. 16px between tiles, 24px tile padding, 40px between sections.
Density
Medium. Two columns of long project tiles, a row of standing tiles for the chart.
Corners
12px on tiles and panels, round pips, pill buttons.
Borders
3px black tile outlines with a 2px black or ivory centre line between halves.
Shadows
Tiles sit on a bone lip, 0 4px 0 #ddd5c3, plus a soft 0 8px 16px rgba(22,21,19,0.08).
Icons
Solid pips and simple shapes in currentColor. Round pip for On track, diamond for At risk, cross for Behind and overdue. People show as tiny dominoes with their initials.
Tone
Short and steady, such as Line of play and Ten pips per tile.
