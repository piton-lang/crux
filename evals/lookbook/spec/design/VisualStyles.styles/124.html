Rules of the house

Spacing
8px unit; 16px row padding, 24px between seals, 48px between sections.
Density
Ceremonial and centred. One register of six rows, a chart and six attestations.
Corners
Square everywhere. Only seals, monograms and the bell are round.
Borders
Double frame: 2px ink, then gilt hairlines. Triple-ruled register ends, dotted leaders.
Shadows
None. Inset double lines on buttons and frames stand in for depth.
Icons
Filled seal-like shapes: round seal with a check, diamond with a bar, square with a cross. Line icons at 1.4 stroke for tools.
Tone
Formal and ceremonial: “Brightwork hereby greets”, “Be it noted that 4 tasks are overdue”.
