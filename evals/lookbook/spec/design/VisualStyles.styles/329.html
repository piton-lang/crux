Style 329 · Hi-fi receiver

The player is a stacked hi-fi component in brushed dark aluminium, with a blue backlit LCD for the song and lyrics, a machined volume knob ringed by a lit arc, little LED push buttons and a tuner dial whose presets are the queue.

Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
8px unit. 24px faceplate padding, 8px gaps between components in the rack.
Density
Medium. Each component holds one function, labels are tiny and dense like real gear.
Corners
4px plates, 2px buttons and LCD windows, round knob.
Borders
A 1px light top bevel and 1px dark bottom bevel on every plate and button, LCD windows inset 2px.
Shadows
Inset shadow in LCD windows, a 0 2px 0 black step under each button, a soft blue glow from LCD text.
Icons
Small solid transport glyphs above engraved caps. State is a round LED plus the word ON or OFF.
Focus
A 2px amber outline offset 2px, matching the LEDs.
Tone
Engraved equipment words, such as Volume, Input, Presets, Text, Shuffle on.
