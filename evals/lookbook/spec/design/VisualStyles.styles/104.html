System

Spacing
A 4px unit on a 24px stud grid. Panels pad 20px, gaps of 12 and 24px.
Density
Medium. Big bricks, clear rows, one project per row.
Corners
4px on every brick, round studs and round avatar studs.
Borders
None. Rows divide with a 3px stud-grey groove.
Shadows
A 6px darker inset lip on each brick bottom plus a hard 3px drop. No blur.
Icons
Solid filled shapes. Round stud for On track, triangle for At risk, square for Behind, cross for overdue, always with the word.
Tone
Cheerful build talk, such as Let's build and One brick is one task.
