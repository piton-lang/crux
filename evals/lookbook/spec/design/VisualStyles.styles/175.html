System

Layout
Stacked full-width bands inside a 1232px container. A navy top bar holds a square outline mark and the Brightwork wordmark, the six nav items as horizontal tabs with a white underline on Home, then a white search field, a white New project button, an outlined bell and Mara Okafor at right. A white sub-tab bar beneath holds Overview, My tasks, Timeline and Workload with a navy underline on Overview, and the date with a calendar icon at right. The greeting sits on the page with the overdue line and a Review them link. A full-width stone band runs edge to edge with four figures split by vertical rules, each a caps label, a 44px navy number and a short note, overdue in red with a clock. Then three equal columns of white panels with a 3px navy top rule. Projects lists six rows of name, status, category and due, and a 6px bar. The chart panel shows fourteen bars, hatched outlined last week and solid navy this week, over a small table of week totals and change. Activity lists six entries with square initials. Below 1040px the nav wraps under the brand and activity spans two columns. Below 760px the nav becomes a three by two grid, figures go two by two and panels stack.
Spacing
An 8px unit. 24px gutters between columns, 20px figure padding, 12 to 18px row padding, 28px above the columns.
Density
Medium. Figures are large and airy, the columns beneath are compact.
Corners
Nearly square. 3px panels, buttons and fields, round avatar, square initials.
Borders
1px warm grey panel borders and row rules, a 3px navy top rule on panels, darker vertical rules in the band.
Shadows
None. Hierarchy comes from the navy bar, the stone band and rules.
Icons
Line icons at 2px stroke, 18px. Status is text with a check circle for On track, a triangle for At risk, a crossed square for Behind and a clock for overdue.
Focus
A 3px navy outline offset 2px with a white gap, white and inset on the navy bar.
Tone
Formal and businesslike, with caps labels such as Due this week and Hours logged and plain links such as All projects.
