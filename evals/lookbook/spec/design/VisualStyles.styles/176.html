System

Layout
A slim top line in a 1120px container with a small violet diamond and the wordmark, the six nav items as quiet text pills with Home in a grey pill, and a bell and Mara Okafor at right. Then a centred 720px hero with lots of air above it, the date in muted text, a 34px greeting, a red overdue line with a Show them link, and the command field, 60px tall with a 14px radius, a search icon, the placeholder Search or type a command, and Ctrl K keycaps at the right end. Under it three centred action buttons each carry a keycap, a violet New project with N, Overdue tasks with G O and Log time with T. Next a full-width strip of four figures between hairlines, each a number followed by a lowercase label on one line. Below sit two unboxed lists side by side, Projects at three fifths and Recent activity at two fifths, each under a muted heading and hairline. Project rows show name over category, a thin violet progress line with percent, due date and status. Activity rows show the sentence and the time at right. The chart runs full width beneath, airy bars with dashed outlines last week and violet this week. A footer hint reads Press ? for all keyboard shortcuts. Below 960px the lists stack, below 760px figures go two by two and keycaps leave the field.
Spacing
An 8px unit with generous gaps. 72px above the hero, 32px from greeting to field, 40px between sections, 56px between the two lists, 11px row padding.
Density
Sparse at the top, medium in the lists. One focal control and little chrome.
Corners
Soft. 14px on the command field, 8px on buttons and fields, 5px keycaps, 4px bar tops, round initials.
Borders
Hairlines only, under rows and around the figure strip. No cards. The command field has a 1px grey edge.
Shadows
One soft violet-tinted shadow under the command field. Nothing else is raised.
Icons
Thin line icons at 1.5px stroke, 18px, 22px in the field. Status icons use 2px stroke, a check circle for On track, a triangle for At risk, a crossed square for Behind and a clock for overdue, always with the word. Keycaps carry shortcuts.
Focus
A 2px white gap then a 2px violet ring as a box shadow, and the command field gains a violet edge and a soft violet halo.
Tone
Calm and terse with lowercase figure labels such as tasks due this week, and command verbs such as Show them and Log time.
