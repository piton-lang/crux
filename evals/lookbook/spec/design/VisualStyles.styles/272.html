Spacing, density and surface

Spacing
8px unit. 36px gutters and column gaps, 8px between leaf rows, 14 by 16px row padding.
Density
Medium. Eight rows hang from one vine with room for the line art.
Corners
Leaf shaped, 16px on two opposite corners and 3px on the others. The details panel is a 170px arch over 18px feet.
Borders
1px vine green on panels and rows, a 2px vine line down the list and the nav, a petal edge on the selected row.
Shadows
None. Light comes from the moon, cream and petal accents.
Icons
18px line icons in leaf green. Each row hangs from a leaf, folders from a bud, the selected file from a flower. Status pairs a filled star, two people or an up arrow ring with an italic word.
