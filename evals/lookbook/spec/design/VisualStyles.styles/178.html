Style 178 · Focus column

The whole dashboard as one calm 720px column read top to bottom in priority order, a sentence that names what matters, then numbered sections set in a bookish serif on off-white paper with ink blue accents and rules instead of boxes.

Palette

  • paper #faf8f3the off-white page
  • ink #1b2433text, section rules and the nav top rule
  • inkBlue #1d3c78the wordmark, links, figures, section numbers, the primary button, progress lines, this week bars and focus
  • muted #5b5f68meta, captions, times and the date kicker
  • rule #d8d3c7hairlines between rows and figures
  • lastWeek #8a8f9coutlines of last week bars
  • ok #2f6b3aOn track text and check circle
  • risk #8a4a00At risk text and triangle
  • bad #a3261bBehind and overdue text, crossed square and clock

Two stacks. Reading text uses Charter, Bitstream Charter, Iowan Old Style, Palatino Linotype, Palatino, Georgia at 17px and 1.6. Meta, nav, buttons and labels use Helvetica Neue, Segoe UI, system-ui, Roboto, Arial at 13 to 15px. The greeting is 44px serif bold at minus 0.02em, the lead 21px serif, section titles 22px serif bold, project names 19px serif bold, figures 40px serif regular in ink blue, and the date kicker and section numbers 13px sans caps tracked 0.08 to 0.14em.

System

Layout
One centred column 720px wide with 16px side padding and nothing beside it. The masthead has the wordmark in ink blue, Bright bold and work italic, with a round outlined bell and Mara Okafor at right. Under it the six nav items run as one line of sans text links between a 2px ink rule above and a hairline below, Home underlined in ink blue. Then an underlined search field and a New project button on one line. The greeting follows with generous space, a caps date kicker, a 44px serif Good morning, Mara. and a lead sentence that starts with 4 tasks are overdue in red with a clock and names the behind and at risk projects. Five numbered sections follow, each titled under a 2px ink rule. 01 Needs attention lists the overdue tasks, Billing migration and Mobile app 2.0 with icons in the margin and open task counts. 02 This week shows the four figures as large serif numbers in a row split by hairlines. 03 All projects is an ordered list, most urgent first, with an index number, the serif name, the status at right, meta with category, tasks, due date and days left, and a 2px progress line. 04 Tasks completed opens with a sentence of the week totals over square outlined and solid bars. 05 Recent activity puts the time in a left margin column. A closing line ends the page. Below 760px figures go two by two and the margin times move under each entry.
Spacing
An 8px unit with a reading rhythm. 56px above the greeting, 48px between sections, 16px row padding.
Density
Low. One column, one thing after another, wide line height.
Corners
Square. Only the bell, avatar and buttons at 2px or round.
Borders
Rules only. A 2px ink rule over each section and the nav, 1px warm hairlines between rows, an underline for the search field.
Shadows
None.
Icons
Line icons at 1.6px stroke, 18px, set in the text or in a 28px left margin. Status uses a check circle for On track, a triangle for At risk, a crossed square for Behind and a clock for overdue, always with the word.
Focus
A 2px ink blue outline offset 3px on every control and field.
Tone
Written and calm, in full sentences such as Billing migration is 30% done with 28 tasks open and 20 days to go.
