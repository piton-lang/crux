Style 351 · Pager LCD

The chat as a stack of two-way pagers, where every message is its own small backlit LCD frame counted MSG 1/5 in a moulded shell, folders replace the sidebar, and pill shaped hardware keys stand in for buttons.

Spacing, density and depth

Spacing
8px unit. 16px between pagers, 10px 12px inside each LCD.
Density
Low per frame. One message per screen, frames tiled three across.
Corners
22px top and 30px bottom on shells, 6px on LCDs, full pills on keys.
Borders
A 2px black inset bezel on every LCD, dashed unlit lines inside.
Shadows
A soft 10px drop under shells and 2px to 3px ledges under keys. Faint scan lines on the LCD.
Icons
LCD glyphs, ▶ open, ✉ new, ! mention, ● ◐ ○ presence, each beside a word.
Focus
2px dashed lit blue outline offset 3px.
Tone
Clipped pager words, such as MSG 2/5, FR, 4 NEW, 3 RE, IMG ATTACHED and TX>.
