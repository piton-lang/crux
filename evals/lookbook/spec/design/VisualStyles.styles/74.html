Spacing, corners and depth

Spacing
8px unit. 16px between bezels, 20px padding inside them, 10px between keys and tapes.
Density
Medium. Few large instruments, small captions, readouts carry the weight.
Corners
14px on bezels, 6px on displays, 4px on keys and fields, 2px on captions.
Borders
1px grey rim on bezels and displays, 1px ink on dials and tapes, dashed rows in logs.
Shadows
Raised bezels with a white top highlight and a 2px lip; displays and fields are inset.
Icons
Instrument drawings only: dials with needles, tape gauges with a magenta bug, an attitude ball. Status captions pair a word with an open circle, a solid triangle or a solid square.
Tone
