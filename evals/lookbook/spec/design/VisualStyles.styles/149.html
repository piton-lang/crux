Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
An 8px unit. A 36px printed border, 28 to 32px inside the field, 14px row padding.
Density
Medium. Panels sit edge to edge like blocks on one cloth, split by stripes rather than gaps.
Corners
Square everywhere, only dots and the avatar are round.
Borders
A wide repeat border of indigo butas, 12px madder stripes with cotton diamonds between panels, dashed indigo lines between figures.
Shadows
None, except a 2px indigo offset under the primary button like a misaligned second block.
Icons
Stamped shapes passed through a slight roughening filter. Status uses a six-petal flower for On track, a triangle for At risk and a square with an inner square for Behind, always with the word.
Tone
Quiet workshop voice, such as one printed dot for every tenth of the work.
