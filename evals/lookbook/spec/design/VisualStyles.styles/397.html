Style 397 · Sorting office

A postal sorting room in green enamel and brass, where folders are a wall of pigeonholes filling with slips, messages are tagged dockets on a belt, and the open message is a routing form.

Space, shape and tone

Spacing
8px unit. 24px gutters, 10px between dockets, 3px steel gaps between pigeonholes.
Density
Medium high. Nine pigeonholes, six dockets with stamps, a full form.
Corners
Square everywhere. Dockets have a pointed tag end with a punched hole.
Borders
2 to 3px steel frames and rules. Label stamps dashed.
Shadows
Only inset shadow at the top of each pigeonhole to make it deep.
Icons
Square capped 2px icons. Unread is a red bordered stamp with a solid triangle, Starred a brass stamp with a filled star, Encl. a mint stamp with a clip. Slip bars show how full a folder is.
Focus
3px brass outline offset 2px. Tag shaped dockets get a brass edge plus a 3px inset brass frame instead.
Tone
Postal shorthand in capitals. Belt, Docket, Encl. 1, Find a slip.
