Rules

Spacing
8px unit. 16px cell padding, 40 to 48px between the brief, the table and the lower band.
Density
Medium. One table carries the detail; prose and facts carry the headline.
Corners
Nearly square, 3px on controls, 0 on the table, tracks and swatches.
Borders
Warm 1px hairlines between rows; a 2px ink rule tops the chart and activity sections.
Shadows
None. Depth comes from paper against white.
Icons
1.6px line icons at 18px. Status uses solid shapes beside the word, dot for On track, triangle for At risk, square for Behind.
Tone
