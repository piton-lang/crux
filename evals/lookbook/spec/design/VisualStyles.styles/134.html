Style 134 · Shipping labels

Every panel is a white parcel label stuck on kraft cardboard, with thick black rules, barcodes, tracking codes and handling pictograms for status.

  • Tape#c9ae7f · tape strips, decoration only
  • Label#ffffff · every panel and field

  • Grey#4d4d4d · units and type notes
  • Green#1d6a33 · On track, this-side-up
  • Amber#8f5300 · At risk, fragile glass
  • Red#b8141b · Behind, overdue warning
  • Marker#ffe14a · focus halo only
