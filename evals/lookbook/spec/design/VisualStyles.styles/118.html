Style 118 · Postage stamps

The dashboard is a page in a stamp album: numbers are denominations, projects are a perforated souvenir sheet with an overprinted status, and the chart is a coil of one-task stamps.

Surface rules

Spacing
8px unit. 9px perforated margin round every stamp, 16px between stamps, 36px before sections.
Density
Medium. Each project is one self-contained stamp, read at a glance.
Corners
Square. Edges are perforated: 10px pitch holes cut into the margin.
Borders
2–3px frame in the stamp colour inside the perforations. The album page has a thin rule and a double outer rule.
Shadows
A 1px drop shadow so stamps lift off the album.
Icons
Engraved line vignettes per team. Overprint bars: filled dot for On track, triangle on a dashed bar for At risk, square on carmine for Behind, clock for overdue.
Tone
Philatelic and calm, such as The project issue, Recent post and denomination.
Focus
3px ultramarine outline offset 4px.
