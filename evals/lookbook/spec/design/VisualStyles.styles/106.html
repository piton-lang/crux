Style 106 · Cross-stitch

The dashboard is an embroidered sampler on aida cloth, where every bar and border is counted out one cross stitch at a time on an 8px grid.

Palette

  • Aida#f7f3e8The cloth, with #e4dccb holes
  • Fine linen#fffdf8Count panel, chart, fields
  • Navy floss#1d2d50Text, this week, primary button
  • Backstitch#23395fDashed rules and outlines
  • Red floss#a3263aCurrent nav, overdue, Behind, focus
  • Green floss#2e6b3aOn track and heading motifs
  • Gold floss#8a5a00At risk text, #a06d12 crosses for last week
  • Hoop wood#c99a5bThe greeting hoop, decoration only
  • Grey floss#4d4a44Meta, dates and times

Mono stack: ui-monospace, SF Mono, Menlo, Consolas, Liberation Mono. Serif stack: Baskerville, Baskerville Old Face, Hoefler Text, Georgia.

System

Spacing
An 8px stitch unit. Every size and gap is a whole number of stitches, 8, 16, 24 and 40px.
Density
Medium. Projects are single stitched rows, numbers sit in a 2 by 2 panel.
Corners
Square everywhere except the round greeting hoop.
Borders
2px solid navy for panels and 2px dashed backstitch for rules. A cross-stitched border in red, navy and green frames the sampler.
Shadows
None. The cloth is flat.
Icons
Pixel motifs on an 8 by 8 grid. A stitched tick for On track, a solid triangle for At risk, a hollow square with a centre for Behind, an X for overdue.
Tone
Quiet needlework words, such as Latest stitches and 1 cross = 1 task.
