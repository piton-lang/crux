Spacing, density and depth

Spacing
8px unit with generous steps of 24 and 40px. Mats have 24 to 28px padding.
Density
Sparse and quiet, with even rhythm and plenty of empty mat.
Corners
Square mats and 2px controls. Only bamboo is rounded.
Borders
8px heri cloth on the two long sides of each mat, 1px straw rules inside, a 4px dark beam edge.
Shadows
None. Depth comes from the woven straw texture and the bamboo gradient.
Icons
Simple natural shapes. A leaf for On track, an outlined triangle for At risk, a solid square for Behind, a clock ring for Overdue.
Tone
Calm and brief, such as This week, Fourteen days and Recent.
