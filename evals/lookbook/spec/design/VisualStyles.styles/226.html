Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
8px unit with generous air. 40px gutters, 18px row padding, 48 to 56px between blocks.
Density
Low to medium. Two columns, a today column on the left and the work on the right.
Corners
Square everywhere. Only the avatar is round.
Borders
1px #262626 separators between rows, figures and columns, nothing heavier. Fields are a single underline.
Shadows
None. Pure black cannot carry a shadow.
Icons
16px thin line icons at 1.25 stroke, used only for status, search, bell and overdue. Status is a check circle, a triangle or a crossed square.
Tone
Terse and calm. Good morning, Mara. Projects. Activity.
