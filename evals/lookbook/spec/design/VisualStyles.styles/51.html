Style sheet

Style 51 · 50s diner

A retro American diner menu in cherry red and mint, with script headings, a checkerboard band, chrome trim and dotted price leaders that make choosing a plan feel like ordering lunch.

Palette

  • Cream#fffaf0The page
  • Vanilla#fff3dcThe menu card
  • Cherry#c41e3aScript titles, prices, primary button, table head
  • Cherry shade#8f1028Pressed lip under red buttons
  • Mint#a8e6cfHeader band, free note, text shadow
  • Mint pale#dff5ecFeatured plan, striped rows, this sheet
  • Teal#1d6b55Small caps labels, secondary button, ticks
  • Cocoa#3a2e2aBody text, outlines, checkerboard
  • Cocoa soft#6a5a52Italic notes, captions
  • Chrome#fbfcfd → #8e98a3Menu rim and toggle track

The fixtures

Spacing
8px unit. 24px between menu items, 36 to 40px card padding, 72px between sections.
Density
Medium. Plans read as stacked menu lines rather than cards side by side.
Corners
Round and friendly. 28px menu rim, 20px table card, 16px swatches, fully round pills and buttons, speech bubbles with one sharp corner.
Borders
2px cocoa outlines, dotted leaders and dividers, a double cherry rule under the menu title, a cherry and cocoa checkerboard band.
Shadows
One soft drop under the menu, 0 10px 24px at 18 percent cocoa. A hard mint offset behind the table. Buttons carry a pressed bottom lip.
Icons
Few and sign-like. A star badge logo, a starburst for Most popular, ticks in filled teal circles, a Q disc on questions, plus and minus toggles.
Focus
A 3px dashed cherry outline offset 4px. Fields get a white gap then a 2px cherry ring.
Tone
Warm counter talk, such as Today's plans, Ask the counter and What comes on each plate.
