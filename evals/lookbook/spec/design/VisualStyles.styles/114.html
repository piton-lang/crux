Style 114 · Whiteboard

The dashboard is a dry-erase board in an aluminium frame, written up in four marker colours, with magnets for navigation and faint eraser smudges left behind.

Palette

  • Wall#e4e1da page behind the board
  • Board#fbfcfc writing surface, fields, nav labels
  • Aluminium#a7aeb5 frame and marker tray, decorative
  • Black marker#1c2024 text, rules, last week bars
  • Blue marker#1d4fb3 brand, headers, names, this week, At risk, focus
  • Red marker#bd232b overdue, Behind, current nav ring
  • Green marker#1c6a37 On track, hours, sheet labels
  • Ghost#5d656d dates, captions, faded writing
  • Magnet yellow#f1c22e primary button with black text

Surface rules

Spacing
8px unit. 24px around the board, 20px zone padding, 10px table rows.
Density
Medium. One board, three zones split by drawn lines, nothing boxed in cards.
Corners
Uneven hand corners such as 10px 4px 12px 5px on drawn boxes; round magnets and pill buttons; 6px on the frame.
Borders
3px marker strokes for zone dividers and axes, 2px for boxes, dashed 2px for the search box and activity rows.
Shadows
Only physical things cast one: magnets, nav labels, the frame. The button has a 3px hard black lip.
Icons
Line icons drawn at 2.4px stroke in the marker colour. Circled tick for On track, triangle with mark for At risk, crossed box for Behind, clock for overdue.
Tone
Friendly team-room shorthand, such as Morning, Mara! and Latest scribbles.
Focus
3px blue marker outline offset 3px on every control.
