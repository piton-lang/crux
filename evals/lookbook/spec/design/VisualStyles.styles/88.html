Spacing, shape and voice

Spacing
8px unit in large steps. 120px between rooms, 96px between works, 64px page margins on wide screens.
Density
Very sparse. One fact per frame, one short label per work, emptiness on purpose.
Corners
Square everywhere.
Borders
Thick frames of 14px walnut or gilt, or 6px black, around a 28px mat. Elsewhere only 1px hairlines.
Shadows
A soft drop below each frame, 18px down with negative spread, as if lit from above.
Icons
Almost none. Tiny line marks for status, an open circle for On track, an outlined triangle for At risk, a cross for Behind and a solid square for overdue, each with its word.
Tone
Quiet curatorial captions. Room 2, Projects in progress, Visitors are advised to begin with these.
