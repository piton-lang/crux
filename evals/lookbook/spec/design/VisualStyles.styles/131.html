System

Spacing
8px unit; 18px between sash badges, 24px between project badges and panels.
Density
Roomy and proud. Big round badges, three across, with plenty of canvas between.
Corners
Round badges and pill controls; 14px felt panels; 4 to 6px tapes.
Borders
2px brown outline plus a dashed stitch line 5 or 6px inside every panel, tape and badge.
Shadows
A small drop under sewn badges and the sash so they sit on the fabric.
Icons
Chunky 3px line pictures inside badges; status patches are a round, a triangle and a square, each with a stitch edge.
Tone
Warm and encouraging, like a troop leader: Good afternoon, Mara!, Badges in progress, Troop log.
