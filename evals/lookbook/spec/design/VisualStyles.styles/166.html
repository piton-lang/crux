Spacing, density and depth

Spacing
8px unit. 10px between cards, 16 to 24px between sections, a 32px left inset that clears the clipped corner.
Density
Dense and data-literal. Rows of tiny slots and printed digits, with one big typed number per field.
Corners
Every card has its top-left corner clipped at 45 degrees, 22px on large cards and 14px on strips, plus 6px rounding elsewhere. Tabs round 8px on top.
Borders
2px brown field dividers, a 1px rule above column numbers, outlined unpunched slots, 2px currentColor status boxes.
Shadows
Only a 1px brown edge under each card, like stacked stock.
Icons
Hole shapes. A ring for On track, a solid triangle for At risk, a solid oblong for Behind and a diamond with a bang for Overdue, always beside the caps word.
Tone
Data-processing jargon, such as Keypunch, Operator, Punch new project and Project deck.
