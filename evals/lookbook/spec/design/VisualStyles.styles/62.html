Form and finish

Spacing
8px unit. 14px between windows, 30px arch headroom, 12px between entries, 32px between areas.
Density
Ceremonial. Seven tall windows in a row on wide screens, entries centred and spaced generously.
Corners
Round arched tops on windows and the lancet, leaf corners of 20px and 4px on buttons and fields.
Borders
Double rules everywhere: a 1.5px old gold line with a 1px gilt line inset 5 to 8px.
Shadows
None. Depth comes from inset double rules and a gold leaf gradient.

1.5 stroke botanical line icons at text size. Four petal flower for Team, diamond for Deadline, arched door for Out of office. Whiplash corner ornaments frame the page.
