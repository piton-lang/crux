Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
8px unit. 28px between the note, mini notes and panels, 24 to 28px inside frames.
Density
Medium. One ornate note, two mini notes, five series lines and five register lines.
Corners
None. Ovals and rosettes are the only curves.
Borders
Layered. A thin engraving border, a 6px double inner frame, wavy guilloche bands top and bottom, double rules beside headings and dotted dividers.
Shadows
None. Depth comes from fine line work.
Icons
Engraving as ornament, spirograph medallions, a fern in an oval frame. Status uses outline circle check, ruled square and triangle beside italic words, and the exceeded meter switches from horizontal green lines to vertical rust lines.
Focus
A 2px ochre outline offset 3px with a fine green outer ring.
