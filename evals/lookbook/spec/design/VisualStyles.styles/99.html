Spacing, density and finish

Spacing
8px unit. 24px between cards, 16px inside a card, 40px between sections.
Density
Roomy. Six cards in a single row on a wide table, two rows of three on tablets.
Corners
10px on cards and panels, round chips, avatars and the card back initials.
Borders
1px warm grey card edge with an inner hairline frame 6px in.
Shadows
Cards cast 0 2px 0 felt shade plus 0 8px 18px rgba(30,50,30,0.18). The hand cards lift more.
Icons
The four suit shapes in solid fill. Club for On track, diamond for At risk, spade for Behind and overdue. People show as card backs with initials.
Tone
Card room words in plain sentences, such as The deal, Chips won and Play those first.
