Spacing, density, corners and depth

Spacing
An 8px unit in big steps, 24, 32, 48, 96px. Sections breathe with 96px above and below
Density
Sparse and ceremonial. One idea per band, everything centred on a vertical axis
Corners
Square. Curves only as large arches on the hero frame and swatches, and stepped ziggurat tops on panels
Borders
Double gold hairlines everywhere, as a line, a 4 to 6px gap, then a line. Black bars carry a doubled gold edge
Shadows
None. Depth comes from stacked frames and black bands
Ornament
Sunburst rays behind the hero, a fan on the quote band, diamond rules between headings, roman numeral medallions
Icons
Geometric filled shapes in currentColor at text size. Status uses a diamond for On track, a triangle for At risk and a crossed square for Overdue, always with the word
Focus
A 2px jet outline offset 3px inside a 6px gold halo. On black, a 2px gold outline. Fields get a gold underline and a cream fill
Tone
Formal and declarative in spaced capitals, such as What Brightwork does, with italic serif lines for warmth
