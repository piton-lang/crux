spacing, corners and depth

spacing
4px white gaps between tiles · 16 by 20px tile padding · 32px page gutter above the board, none beside it
density
Sparse type, big blocks. One fact line per tile.
corners
0 everywhere
borders
None. Fields carry a 3px inset bottom bar so they still read as fields.
shadows
None. Depth comes only from tile colour steps, a darker head above lighter tiles.
icons
Square-cap 1.5 stroke glyphs at text size, white on tiles, never boxed. Overdue is a triangle, done a tick.
tone
Everything lowercase and terse: “add task”, “at risk”, “no due date”. People keep their capitals.
