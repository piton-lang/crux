Style 117 · Passport stamps

The dashboard is an open passport: a holder page with the user's details and contents, and a visa page where each project is an inked entry stamp whose shape and mark give its status.

Palette

  • Table#e7e5ea page behind the book
  • Cover#3a2a4a thin book edge, decorative only
  • Visa paper#eef3ee pages under the guilloche
  • Security teal#1d5f63 guilloche lines at 10 percent, microprint, section labels
  • Ink#1d2833 text and machine zone
  • Muted#4a5662 field keys, times, last week
  • Violet ink#5a2a86 primary button, current page, this week, focus
  • Green ink#1f6340 On track round stamp with a tick
  • Blue ink#1f4b8f At risk double frame with a triangle
  • Red ink#a1262c Behind leaf stamp with a crossed square, overdue notice

Surface rules

Spacing
8px unit. 28–32px page margins, 18–20px between stamps.
Density
Medium and scattered. Stamps sit loosely on a grid, each turned 3 to 6 degrees.
Corners
Book 14px, pages 8px. Stamp shape is the status: round, square double frame, or a leaf with 28px and 6px corners.
Borders
3px ink frames, 6px double for At risk, hairline teal for summary boxes and observation rules.
Shadows
Only the gutter: an inset shade where the pages meet, and a soft drop under the book.
Icons
Solid ink marks: tick for On track, filled triangle for At risk, crossed filled square for Behind, clock for overdue. A compass rose emblem.
Tone
Travel document words, such as Contents, Entries, Observations and Given name.
Focus
2px violet outline offset 3px with a soft violet halo.
