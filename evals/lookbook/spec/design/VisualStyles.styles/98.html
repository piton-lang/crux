Spacing, density and finish

Spacing
8px unit. 16px inside squares and chips, 24px card padding, 32px between bands.
Density
Medium. Each project is one lane of ten squares, the side column holds small cards.
Corners
12px on cards and dice, 6px on track squares, pill buttons, round pawns.
Borders
3px ink outlines on cards, dice and buttons. Track squares have 2px ink edges.
Shadows
A chunky 0 4px 0 ink lip under cards, dice and buttons, like moulded plastic.
Icons
Solid game shapes with ink outlines. Pawns for people, pips on dice, a chequered flag at the finish. Play arrow for On track, pause bars for At risk, rewind for Behind and overdue.
Tone
Game rules language, such as Your move, Turn log, Miss a turn and Go back.
