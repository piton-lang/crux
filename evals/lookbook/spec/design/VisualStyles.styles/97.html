Spacing, density and finish

Spacing
8px unit. 24px panel padding, 32px between panels.
Density
Loud and roomy. Few items per panel, each one billed large.
Corners
16px on panels, round rings and avatars, ribbon ends cut in a swallowtail notch.
Borders
3px navy outlines on every panel, button and field.
Shadows
Hard 5px offset navy shadow, no blur, like a misprinted second colour.
Icons
Chunky solid shapes with navy outlines. A pennant for On track, a diamond for At risk, a stop octagon for Behind and overdue, a megaphone for announcements.
Tone
Ringmaster patter with exclamation marks, such as Step right up, Presenting and Hold your breath.
