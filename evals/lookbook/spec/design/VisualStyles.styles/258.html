Spacing, density and depth

Spacing
8px unit. 8px seams between every slab, 24px slab padding, 8px between tiles and fields.
Density
Chunky. Big 64px tool blocks, 104px layer tiles, 26px figures.
Corners
None. Every slab, tile, field and button is square.
Borders
Seams instead of borders. 2px ink outlines on secondary buttons and avatars, a hazard stripe tape over the status slab.
Shadows
Inset shadows sink fields and tie holes. Slabs themselves are flat with a faint noise grain.
Icons
20px line icons at 2.4 stroke with square ends. Selected is a circled check, Collapsed a right chevron, Saved a cloud with a check.
Focus
4px solid safety yellow outline, ink on yellow fills.
Tone
Site signage. Stencil caps such as POSITION / SIZE, EXPORT HERO, SAVED 2 MINUTES AGO.
