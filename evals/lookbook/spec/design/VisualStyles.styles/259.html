Spacing, density and depth

Spacing
8px unit. 20px between floating pieces, 20px card padding, 8px between chips.
Density
Light to medium. Everything floats with black showing between.
Corners
Very round. 22px cards, 14px rows, 12px chips, full capsules for bars and buttons.
Borders
A 1px iridescent foil rim on every capsule, card, the selected row and the comment. Plain 1px lines inside.
Shadows
Soft black 0 20px 50px under cards and a faint cyan bloom under the frame.
Icons
18px round line icons at 1.5 stroke, in 30px tiles in the layer list. Selected is a circled check, Collapsed a right chevron, Saved a cloud with a check.

2px cyan outline offset 3px with a 6px cyan haze.
