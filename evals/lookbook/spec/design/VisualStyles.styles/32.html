Style 32 · Zen ink

Japanese ink-brush calm, one sumi stroke and one red sun above a narrow column of hairline fields, with the brand written vertically down the edge and space doing most of the work.

Palette

  • Rice paper#fbfaf6 · the page
  • Sumi#1c1b19 · text, the brush stroke, field lines, the primary button
  • Wash#5e5b55 · labels, small text, the or divider
  • Mist#d9d6ce · resting hairlines between rows
  • Pale#efede6 · the half-height wash behind a focused field
  • Sun#c0392b · the single red dot, used nowhere else

Spacing, corners and depth

Spacing
An 8px unit used in large steps. 32px between fields, 48px between groups, 96px above the screen, 220px of empty paper above the heading.
Density
Very sparse. A 340px column on a 1200px page, nothing beside it but the vertical brand.
Corners
Square buttons and fields. Only the sun, the radio-style checkbox and the sign-in marks are round.
Borders
1px hairlines under fields and rows only. A dashed underline marks the field in error.
Shadows
None. Depth comes from the brush stroke alone.
Icons
Almost none. A hand-made SVG brush stroke, ringed initials on sign-in rows, an arrow →, a ✕ before the error and a ✓ for done.
Tone
Very few words, calm and unhurried. Labels whisper in widely tracked small type.
