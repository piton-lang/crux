Spacing, corners and depth

Spacing
8px unit · 16px between cards · 24px between columns · 24px screen padding
Density
Medium. A whole board fits one laptop screen.
Corners
Wobbly asymmetric radii such as 255/15/225/15 over 15/225/15/255, so no edge is straight
Borders
2px pencil, with a faint second stroke slightly offset and roughened. Columns use a dashed pencil line.
Shadows
None, except a blue pen offset outline behind the primary button
Icons
Hand-drawn 2px round strokes at text size. Overdue is a triangle with !, Done a tick, placeholders a crossed box.
Tone
Plain sketch notes in sentence case: “Add task”, “type to search…”, “5 pts”
