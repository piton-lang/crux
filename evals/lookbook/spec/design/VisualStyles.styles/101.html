Style 101 · Chess notation

The dashboard kept like a tournament scoresheet, with board files for navigation, the totals on a checkered rank, projects in a standings table with chess pieces and annotation marks, and activity written as numbered moves.

Spacing, density and finish

Spacing
8px unit. 8px table cell padding, 40px between blocks.
Density
Dense and orderly, like a scoresheet. Tables carry most of the content.
Corners
Square everywhere.
Borders
1px ink rules for tables and a 2px ink rule under each block heading. The summary rank has a 2px ink frame.
Shadows
None.
Icons
Unicode chess pieces for teams and the brand king. Annotation marks drawn in SVG, ! in a circle for On track, ?! in a diamond for At risk, ?? in a solid square for Behind and overdue.
Tone
Terse tournament shorthand with a plain sentence beside it, such as Check, Standings and Moves.
