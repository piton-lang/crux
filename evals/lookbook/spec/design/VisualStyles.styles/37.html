Style 37 · Claymorphism

Spacing, corners and depth

Spacing
8px unit · 20px between cards · 28px between trays · 18px card padding
Density
Low. Big soft shapes with lots of air.
Corners
36px trays · 32px panels · 28px cards · fully round buttons, fields, chips, counts and avatars
Borders
None on clay. Fields get a 1.5px #c9bdea ring so they read as wells.
Shadows
Raised clay is 10px 12px 24px grape at 18% outside, plus a dark inner shadow bottom right and a white inner highlight top left. Trays and fields are pressed in with the inner shadows reversed.
Icons
Chunky filled rounded glyphs at text size. Overdue is a filled triangle, Done a filled circle tick, no due date a circle with a dash. Avatars are glossy clay balls.
Tone
Warm and plain sentence case: “Find a task”, “Add task”, “5 pt”.
