Style 395 · Focus inbox

The inbox leads with only the few messages that need you, set large in a quiet serif inside vast dark space, and lets everything else shrink to one soft line each.

Space, shape and tone

Spacing
8px unit used in big steps. 96px above the greeting, 72 to 120px between sections, 32px inside cards.
Density
Very sparse. Two cards, four one-line rows and one letter. Nothing else on screen.
Corners
24px focus cards, fully round buttons, square rows.
Borders
1px #2a2b30 hairlines. The open card trades its edge for apricot.
Shadows
None at all.
Icons
Very few, thin 1.5 stroke at text size. New is a hollow ring plus the word New, starred a filled star plus Starred.
Focus
2px apricot outline offset 4px. Fields turn their underline apricot as well.
Tone
A calm human sentence up top, then single words. Can wait, Open, New.
