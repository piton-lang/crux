Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
8px unit on a 24px graph grid. 14px between log entries, 20px between note cards, 28px between columns.
Density
Medium. Six entries in the log, four note cards beside it.
Corners
Nearly square, 2px. The route sheet tilts by 0.6 degrees, tape strips by 3.
Borders
1px slate edges, a 3px left rule on every entry, solid ice when booked and dashed signal orange when not. Dashed rules in tables.
Shadows
Only under the tilted route sheet. Pale tape strips hold the cards.
Icons
Field marks. Booked is a solid flag, Not booked a dashed empty square, Flight a plane. Entry bullets are diamonds, hollow for unbooked. Camps are triangles. Done a ticked box, To do an empty box.
Tone
Expedition plain speech. Log entries, Conditions observed, Kit list, Provisions fund.
