Style 152 · Stained glass

The dashboard is one leaded church window, with every panel a pane of pale jewel glass, black lead cames as the gaps between them, pointed lancets for the totals and a rose window beside the greeting.

Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
An 8px unit. The grid gap is the lead, 8px between major panes and 6px inside them, 14 to 24px padding in each pane.
Density
Medium. A twelve column window with no empty wall between panes.
Corners
Square panes with 3px softening. Lancets and chart bars have pointed arch tops, tabs and swatches have round tops.
Borders
Black lead everywhere, 8px between panes, 6px under pane headings, 2 to 3px around controls and inside the chart.
Shadows
None. Each pane carries a soft white highlight in its top left like light through glass.
Icons
Leaded shapes with a black outline. Status uses a roundel for On track, a triangle for At risk and a diamond with a cross for Behind, always with the word, and the project pane is tinted green, amber or red as a second cue.
Tone
Calm and plain, such as Each pane is tinted by its status.
