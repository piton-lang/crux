Spacing, density and depth

Spacing
8px unit. 16px inside the case and between blocks, 8px between keys.
Density
Busy and tactile like a shop counter. Many small windows, every key large enough to hit with a thumb.
Corners
18px on the case, 8px on keys and LCD bezels, 4px on status codes.
Borders
6px dark bezel around LCDs, 2px taupe key edges, dashed lines between item lines and tape entries.
Shadows
Keys sit on a hard 4px bottom ledge and the current key is pressed down to 1px. LCDs have an inset shadow.
Icons
Solid geometric marks. Circle with check for On track, triangle with bang for At risk, square with cross for Behind, octagon with bang for Overdue, always beside a caps word.
Tone
Register shorthand in caps, such as Item lines, Subtotal, PLU lookup and Z report.
