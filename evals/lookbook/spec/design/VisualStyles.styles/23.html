Spacing, density, corners and depth

Rhythm
A 24px baseline. Every line, row and gap is 12, 24 or 48px
Density
Medium-high but airy. Small type, a full ledger on one page
Corners
2px on buttons and fields, 3px on the seal and stamps, 4px tops on index tabs
Borders
1px hairlines · ink under headings, beige between rows, a dashed stitch between pages
Shadows
None. Depth comes from washi tape strips and tabs
Paper
Pale blue ruled lines every 24px on pages and a 24px graph grid on this sheet
Icons
16px thin square-cap line icons. Status stamps are tilted outlines with ○ for On track, △ for At risk and × for Behind
Focus
2px indigo outline offset 2px; fields get a 2px indigo underline and a pale blue fill

Calm, tidy and brief · “Projects ledger”, “Diary”, numbered sections 01 to 04
