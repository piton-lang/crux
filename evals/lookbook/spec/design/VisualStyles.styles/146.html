Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
An 8px unit. 24 to 32px register padding, 20px between registers, 24px between medallions.
Density
Medium. Registers are stacked like bands round a pot, each holding one kind of content.
Corners
Square registers and frieze panels. Round medallions, round pill buttons, pill fields and a round tipped bar chart.
Borders
3px black glaze lines between frieze panels, Greek key bands 24px tall between registers, dotted and tongue bands as lighter dividers.
Shadows
None. Depth is ringed black and terracotta bands.
Icons
Black-figure silhouettes. An amphora fills with glaze to show progress, ivy leaves mark activity. Status uses a filled diamond for On track, an outlined triangle with a mark for At risk and a hollow square for Behind, with the word.
