Style 128 · Anatomy chart

The dashboard as an antique scientific plate in sepia, where projects are segments of a sectioned specimen with lettered leader lines, numbers are labelled measurements and activity reads as numbered field notes.

Conventions

Spacing
8px unit; 24px between sections, 40px between columns, 28px plate margins.
Density
Moderate, like a printed plate: one large figure, a key, then smaller figures and notes.
Corners
Square frames and boxes; round only for label letters, the bell and the figure.
Borders
1px ink plate edge doubled 8px out, double rules top and foot, dotted hairlines between key rows.
Shadows
One soft drop under the plate, as if pinned to a wall. Nothing else.
Icons
Engraved line marks: ring with dot, open triangle, solid square. Leader lines end in small dots.
Tone
Victorian naturalist: Plate XXXI, Observation, Notes from the field, drawn from life.
