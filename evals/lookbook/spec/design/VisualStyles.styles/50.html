Construction

Spacing
8px unit. 20px inside slabs, 40 to 72px between blocks. Plans stagger down by 48px each.
Density
Loud and medium. Few words, huge figures, short bold lists.
Corners
Square, except the single black disc in the hero. Plan heads and swatches cut on a diagonal.
Borders
4px solid black on every box. 2px rules between list items and table rows. 6px red under the table head.
Shadows
Only a hard 6px offset block in black behind the primary button. No blur.
Icons
Solid geometric shapes. Bar and triangle arrows on buttons, triangles as list bullets and included marks, a flat bar for not included.
Focus
4px black outline offset 3px with a cream gap. Fields turn their frame red with a 16px left slab.
Tone
Short commands in capitals, such as Pick one, Plan against plan.
