Spacing, corners and depth

Spacing
8px unit. 9 to 12px cell padding in rows, 14 to 16px between boards.
Density
Medium high. Long boards with one row per project.
Corners
Square signs; housings 4px, flap cells 2px.
Borders
6px grey frame around each board, 2px rules between rows, 2px ink on signs and fields.
Shadows
A flat 3px lip under each housing, no blur.
Icons
Station pictograms in solid ink. Remarks use a disc for On track, a triangle for At risk and a square for Behind beside the flap word.
Tone
Station announcer: Departures, calling at Design, running on time, expect delays, Customer notice.
