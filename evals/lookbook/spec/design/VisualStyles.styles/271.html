Spacing, density and surface

Spacing
4px base. 20px between slabs and columns, 6px between file slabs, 18px slab padding.
Density
Medium dense. Each file is a 52px slab with its meta in columns.
Corners
Chamfered. 12px cuts on the top left and bottom right of slabs, 7px on buttons, fields, badges and tiles.
Borders
No ordinary borders. Inner 1px lines on secondary controls and badges, a 3px lava edge on the current nav item.
Shadows
No drop shadows. Slabs have a 135 degree glass gradient and a faint white sheen, and lava lines glow with a 2px blur.
Icons
18px line icons in muted grey, lava when current or selected. Badges pair a filled star, two people or an up arrow ring with the word.
Focus
2px lava outline, 3px offset, and the chamfer drops away so the outline is whole.
Tone
Short caps labels, such as Drive, Storage and Shared ×4, over plain values.
