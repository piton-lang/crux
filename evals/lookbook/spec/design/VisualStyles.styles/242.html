Style 242 · Velvet theatre

The dashboard is staged inside a theatre, with deep red velvet curtains and a scalloped pelmet framing a spotlit stage, the figures printed on ticket stubs, the projects listed in a gilt programme and the chart drawn as arched gilt columns.

Spacing, density and surfaces

Spacing
An 8px unit. 32px programme padding, 24px between entries, 40px between stage sections.
Density
Low to medium. A staged, ceremonial layout with room to breathe.
Corners
Square panels with arched column tops, notched ticket stubs and a scalloped pelmet.
Borders
Double gilt rules on the programme, 1px gilt rules on panels, a dashed tear line on each stub.
Shadows
The curtains cast deep inner shadows on the stage and a warm spotlight falls from above, nothing on the controls.
Icons
Thin line icons at 1.6px stroke in gilt. Status uses a check circle, a triangle with a bang and a crossed square, always with the word, overdue a clock.
Focus
A 2px gilt outline offset 3px.
Tone
Ceremonial and playful, such as Tonight's programme, Act I and Notices, without overdoing it.
