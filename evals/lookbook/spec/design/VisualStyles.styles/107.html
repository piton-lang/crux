System

Spacing
An 8px unit. Fabric frames are 16 to 20px wide around each muslin centre.
Density
Medium. Blocks touch with no gaps, like a finished quilt top.
Corners
Square, 2px at most on controls.
Borders
A 12px denim binding with a dashed stitch inside, 2px ink edges on centres, 1.5px dashed quilting lines.
Shadows
None. Texture comes from the fabrics.
Icons
Solid shapes with white marks. Ticked circle for On track, triangle for At risk, square with a down arrow for Behind, octagon for overdue.
Tone
Homey and kind, such as Welcome home and News from the sewing circle.
