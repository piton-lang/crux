Spacing, corners and depth

Spacing
Tight, a 4px base. 12px between rows, 24 to 28px window padding.
Density
Compact desktop density, 13px type, 30px controls.
Corners
Fully round pill buttons, 10px top window corners, 6px groups, 4px fields.
Borders
1px grey bevels, darker on top for inset fields. Dividers are a grey line over a white line.
Shadows
A deep soft window shadow, inset field shadows, and gel buttons with a white top highlight and a light blue inner glow below.
Icons
Glossy orbs and gel balls with a white reflection. An octagon with a cross for errors, a tick in the app icon.
Tone
Plain desktop dialog wording with colons after labels, such as Email: and Password:.
