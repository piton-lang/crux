Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
An 8px unit. 24 to 32px between cards and boards, 12px between directory lines.
Density
Medium. Big showy lettering up top, a tidy directory below.
Corners
Square cards and boards, 3 to 6px on the fascia, buttons and fields, round bell and avatar.
Borders
Gold pinstripes inset in the fascia, the window frame and every board, 2px black around show cards, dotted leaders and double rules.
Shadows
Hard offset drops only, gold under black letters and cards, black under the primary button, like painted shade lines.
Icons
Solid painted shapes. Status uses a ticked circle for On track, a triangle with an exclamation for At risk and a crossed square for Behind, with the caps word.
Tone
A friendly shopkeeper, such as Now showing, the full directory and news from the workshop.
