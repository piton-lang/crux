Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
8px unit. 20px between map and list, 10px row padding, 24px gutter, 16px on phones.
Density
Medium. The map carries placement, the keyed list carries the detail.
Corners
6px map frame, 4px buttons and fields, round pins. Swatches are map pin teardrops.
Borders
2px region borders, 1px rules under every list row, dashed contour lines and summary dividers.
Shadows
None. Pins sit on a 3px sea coloured ring so they lift off the land.
Icons
Line icons at 1.9 stroke in the chrome. On the map, pin shape carries state, circle open, diamond date booked, square sent, triangle stalled, ringed circle won.
Focus
A 2px dashed ice outline offset 3px.
Tone
Field and survey words. Territories, Place deal, Field notes, Deals on the map.
