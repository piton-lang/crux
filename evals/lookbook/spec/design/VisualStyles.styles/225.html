Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

8px unit. 12px cell padding, 40 to 48px between sections, 24px gutters.
Density
Medium. Everything is one ruled column, then chart and log side by side.
Corners
2px on buttons and fields. Everything else is square.
Borders
1px hairlines everywhere. #1e2d4d between rows and cells, #2e4370 above and below sections.
Shadows
None. Depth comes only from hairlines and the panel tone of the masthead.
Icons
16px square-capped line icons at 1.5 stroke. Progress is ten ticks. Status is a check circle, a triangle or a crossed square with the word in caps.
