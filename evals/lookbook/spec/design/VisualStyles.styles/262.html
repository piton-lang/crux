Spacing, density and depth

Spacing
8px unit. 16px between panels, 48px around the frame on the stage, 10px between strip cells.
Density
Medium. Layers and readout on the left, the stage wide, all properties in one strip.
Corners
Tight. 6px panels, 4px controls and rows, diamonds for handles.
Borders
1.5px neon outlines, magenta around layers, cyan around properties, dim violet for idle controls.
Shadows
Neon glows only, 10 to 24px in the line colour, never a dark drop shadow.
Icons
18px line icons at 1.8 stroke in cyan. Selected is a circled check, Collapsed a right chevron, Saved a cloud with a check.
Focus
2px cyan outline offset 3px with a cyan glow.
Tone
Arcade cabinet. Italic caps for titles, mono caps readouts such as Position / size.
