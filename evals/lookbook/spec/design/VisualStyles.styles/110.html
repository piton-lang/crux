Style 110 · Sewing pattern

Every panel is a numbered pattern piece on tissue paper, with a dashed cutting line, an inner stitching line, notches and grain arrows, and progress drawn as a grain arrow sewn part way.

  • Tissue#f3e9d2The page, with faint crinkles

  • Green#2f6233On track
  • Amber#7d4e00At risk

System

Spacing
An 8px unit. A 12px seam allowance between the cut line and the stitch line, 28px between pieces.
Density
Medium. Pieces of different sizes laid out on a 12 column cutting table.
Corners
Square, with one big curved corner on some pieces, like an armhole or hem curve.
Borders
2px dashed outer cut line, 1px solid inner stitch line, black triangle notches on edges.
Shadows
None. Tissue lies flat.
Icons
Pattern markings. Filled dot for On track, filled triangle notch for At risk, square in square for Behind, crossed circle for overdue, scissors on every cut line.
Tone
Pattern instructions, such as Cut 1, Finished measurements and Sewing steps.
