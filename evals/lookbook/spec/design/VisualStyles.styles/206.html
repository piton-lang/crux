Spacing, density and form

Spacing
8px unit in large steps. 24px gaps, 28px card padding, 64px between sections.
Density
Low. Six project cards, never a table. Everything fits in two scrolls.
Corners
20px cards, 14 to 16px fields and rows, full pills for buttons, nav and chips.
Borders
Almost none. Hairlines only between activity rows and in the top bar.
Shadows
One soft lift on cards, 0 8px 24px at 7 percent ink.
Icons
24px outline icons. Status uses a filled circle with check, triangle with bang, octagon with a bar.
Focus
A 3px forest outline offset 3px on every control.
Tone
Plain sentences a manager would say aloud, such as Where projects stand and Up 14 on last week.
