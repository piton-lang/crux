8px unit. 22px between signs, alternate signs dropped 28px to hang at different heights, 18px between lower panels.
Density
Medium. Six signs in three columns, then four small panels.
Corners
14px sign faces joined to 12px blades, pill status tags and buttons.
Borders
2px tube borders in each sign colour, dashed for unlit signs. 1px dark edges on panels.
Shadows
Neon glow in the tube colour outside and inside lit signs and around primary controls. No drop shadows.
Icons
2px line icons tinted in the tube colour. Booked is a check circle in a solid pill, Not booked a dashed circle in a dashed pill, Done a check, To do an open circle.
