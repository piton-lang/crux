Spacing, shape and tone

LayoutA phone-width workbook, at most 390px, centred on a grey desk. Top to bottom it has a title bar with the avatar, a toolbar, the formula bar, the grid with a row-number column and columns A to C (a sliver of D on wide screens), a status bar and sheet tabs as the bottom bar.
Spacing4px unit. Cells pad 4px by 6px. No gaps; everything butts against gridlines.
DensityHigh. All eight items plus headers fit on one phone screen.
CornersSquare everywhere, 2px at most on buttons, badge and avatar.
Borders1px gridlines on every cell. The selected cell has a 2px teal border and a square fill handle at the bottom right; its column letter and row number turn teal and bold.
ShadowsNone.
IconsSmall 11 to 18px square-ended line icons. Status cells use conditional fills plus a dot, ring, tick or triangle and a word.
ToneTerse and literal, like column headers: Due, Task, Status, Length. A formula line shows how counts are made.
