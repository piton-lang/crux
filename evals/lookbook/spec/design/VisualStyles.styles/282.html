Spacing, density and depth

Fonts
One book serif, Baskerville, Libre Baskerville, Garamond, Times New Roman. Headings in small caps, meta and statuses in italic.
Spacing
8px unit. 28px between cards, 26px card padding, 12px rows.
Density
Relaxed. Three cards with generous margins.
Corners
14px cards with a 9px inner frame, pills for controls, round medallions.
Borders
1px dull gold outer and inner frames, dotted gold rules between rows.
Shadows
A soft long drop under each card, nothing else.
Icons
1.3px fine line icons in gold medallions. Status is an italic word with a star, people, arrow or checked box. Uploading also shows five moon phase dots.
Tone
Hushed and a little mystical in headings, The files, Drive. Plain in data.
Motifs
A star field, a crescent moon over the title, four star corners on each card, a sun and moon drawing around the preview, a moon in the storage ring.
