Style 93 · Cassette J-card

The dashboard is a home-made mixtape, with the week written on a cassette label, the projects as a hand-lettered track list on a folded J-card with tape counters and reels, and the chart as segmented recording levels.

Spacing, shape and voice

Spacing
8px unit. 10px per track row, 32px between the cassette, card and panels.
Density
Medium. Each track packs a name, a counter, reels and a status in one line.
Corners
14px on the cassette shell, 6px on the label, 3px on keys and fields, square on the J-card.
Borders
1px shell grey on paper, dashed fold lines between card panels, 3px bottom edges on keys like piano keys.
Shadows
One soft drop under the J-card. Keys press down by losing their thick bottom edge.
Icons
Tape deck transport marks. On track is Play, At risk is Pause, Behind is Rewind and overdue is the round Record dot, always with the word.
Tone
A friend's mixtape. this week's mix, Track list, Recording log, Rec 4 tasks are overdue.
