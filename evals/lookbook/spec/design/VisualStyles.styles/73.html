Spacing, shape and tone

Layout
A centred playbill up to 400px. A 3px black border, a cream gap and a hairline make the outer frame, with red corner ornaments on an inner hairline frame. Everything is centred. The header stacks issue number and avatar, The Brightwork in wood type, fleurons, the date in slab and the counts in italic. Meetings list hour boxes beside names. Each task is a centred bill entry with an ordinal, a name, a project line and a notice. The bottom bar sits under a double rule.
Spacing
8px unit. 10px above and below each entry, 18px before section heads.
Density
Medium and theatrical. Large headings with plenty of ornament between.
Corners
Square. Only the avatar, badge and add button are round, each with a ring around it.
Borders
3px double rules, 1px rules between entries, dotted rules between meetings, and section heads flanked by twin hairlines.
Shadows
None on the screen. Buttons in the style sheet use a hard 3px offset ink shadow like a misregistered print.
Icons
Printer's ornaments and glyphs, ❦ ❧ ✥ fleurons, a pointing hand for the current tab and priority, ✦ for today, ○ upcoming, ✓ done and ✗ overdue. Nav icons are thin engraved line drawings.
Tone
Playbill announcer, Featuring 6 tasks & 2 meetings, The first, lasting 30 min.
