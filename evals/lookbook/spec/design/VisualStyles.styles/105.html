System

Spacing
An 8px unit. Patches pad 22px, gaps of 20 and 32px.
Density
Relaxed. Two patches across, plenty of linen between.
Corners
Uneven hand-cut radii between 10 and 34px, every patch different and tilted under one degree.
Borders
No outer border. A 2px dashed running stitch sits 7px inside every shape.
Shadows
A soft 6px blur under each patch and a 2px lip, like felt resting on cloth.
Icons
Filled craft shapes. Two-hole button for On track, triangle for At risk, square with a stitched cross for Behind, diamond with a mark for overdue.
