Spacing, density and surfaces

Spacing
An 8px unit. 4px inside controls, 8 to 12px cell padding, 16px between panels.
Density
High. 40px table rows, 48px header, many facts per screen.
Corners
Square panels. 3px on buttons and fields, round avatar only.
Borders
1px hairlines #d6d6d3 around panels and between rows and KPI cells.
Shadows
None. Depth comes from the page grey behind white panels.
Icons
16px line icons at 1.8px stroke in grey. Status icons are 14px and coloured, circle, triangle and square.
Focus
2px graphite outline offset 2px on every control.
Tone
Terse and factual. Activity log, Status mix, 4 tasks are overdue.
