Spacing, corners and depth

Spacing
8px unit · 16px between cards · 32px page padding · 24px between groups
Density
Very loose. One idea per line, words big enough to read aloud.
Corners
Wobbly elliptical radii (255px 18px 225px 18px / 18px 225px 18px 255px) so no two boxes match
Borders
3px solid ink on every box; dashed 3px for things not done yet
Shadows
One flat offset, 4px 5px 0 at 18% ink, on the book, note and Continue
Icons
Hand-drawn SVG with a displacement filter for crayon wobble and waxy grain; ticks, stars, pencils
Tone
Cheerful and short: "You are here", "Still writing", "Done! Gold star."
