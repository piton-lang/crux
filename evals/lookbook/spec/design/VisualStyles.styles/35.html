Spacing, corners and depth

Spacing
8px unit · 24px between notes · 24px between scraps · 24px scrap padding
Density
Loose. Paper needs room to overlap and tilt.
Corners
Square paper with torn, zigzag or receipt edges cut by clip-path. Round only on the points sticker and Done stamp.
Borders
None on paper. Stamps have 3px ink outlines. Fields use a dashed underline.
Shadows
Paste shadows, drop-shadow 1px 2px 1.5px rgba(40,25,5,.35). Every scrap tilts between -1.4 and 1.2 degrees.
Icons
Bold 2.2 stroke at text size, inside stamps. Overdue and At risk use a triangle, Done a tick in an oval stamp.
Tone
Crafty and short. Typewriter labels such as Who, Due, Notes.
