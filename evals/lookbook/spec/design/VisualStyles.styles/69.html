Style 69 · Boarding pass

Every meeting is a line on a departure board and every task a paper ticket with a torn-off stub, so the day reads like a travel itinerary.

Palette

  • Tarmac#e7e9ec page behind the tickets, notch cut-outs
  • Ticket#fffdf8 ticket body, header, nav strip
  • Stub#f3efe4 the tear-off stub
  • Navy ink#15233f text, header band, gate signs, Due today stamp
  • Slate#4d5a70 field captions, Scheduled stamp
  • Perforation#9aa3b2 dashed tear lines (decorative)
  • Signal orange#b23c0c add button, priority tag, badge, focus
  • Board#1b2433 departure board for meetings
  • Flap#f6f3e8 split-flap digit tiles
  • Cleared green#1d6b45 Done stamp with tick

Spacing, shape and tone

Spacing
8px unit. 12px between tickets, 12 to 14px ticket padding, 28px before each gate sign.
Density
Medium. One ticket per task with three facts each, about five tickets per phone screen.
Layout
A single phone column up to 400px centred on a grey tarmac. Each ticket splits into a main part and a 92px stub on the right, joined by a dashed perforation with round notches top and bottom.
Corners
10 to 14px on tickets and the board, 2 to 3px on stamps and gate signs, round avatar and add button.
Borders
2px dashed perforation lines. Stamps use 1.5 to 2px solid outlines. The overdue ticket gets a 2px red ring.
Shadows
A 1px hard bottom edge plus a soft 0 4px 10px navy at 8 percent, like paper lying on a desk.
Icons
2px stroke line icons with square ends. A paper plane is the brand mark. Status stamps carry a filled dot, a hollow ring, a tick or a triangle.
Tone
Travel words in short caps labels: Passenger, Gate, Departures, Boarding. Task names stay plain sentence case.
