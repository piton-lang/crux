Style 273 · Moonlit zen

Spacing, density and surface

Spacing
8px unit, used generously. 64px gutters and column gaps, 14px row padding, 56px above the moon.
Density
Sparse. One line of meta per file and nothing boxed except the pebble.
Corners
Round and organic. 21px pill buttons, a 48 to 56px pebble, a slightly uneven stone.
Borders
Almost none. Three raked wavy lines between files, a 1px underline on search and fields.
Shadows
None. The selected row has a faint radial glow and its icon sits on a moon stone ringed by two raked circles.
Icons
18px line icons in muted grey, dark on the selected stone. Status words pair a filled star, two people or an up arrow ring.
Focus
1.5px pale gold outline, 5px offset.
Tone
Hushed and brief, such as Details and 64.2 GB of 100 GB under the moon.
