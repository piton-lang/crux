Build rules

Spacing
8px unit, used loosely. 20 to 28px gaps so the shadows have room, 14 to 20px padding.
Density
Medium. Big chunky blocks, about six project cards on a laptop screen.
Corners
Zero. Every corner is sharp.
Borders
3px solid black on every box, field, button, divider and chart bar. 6px black rule above the style sheet.
Shadows
Hard offset 6px 6px 0 black, no blur. Buttons drop into their shadow when pressed.
Layout
Full-width bands. Stat blocks are staggered at different heights and the brand tilts by 2 degrees on purpose.
Icons
Square-cap 3px line icons. Status is a stamp with a solid black circle, triangle or X beside the word.
Tone
Loud and blunt, caps for labels. “4 TASKS OVERDUE. FIX THESE FIRST.”, “VIEW ALL →”.
