Spacing, corners and depth

Spacing
8px unit, generous. 26px between labels, 26 to 34px inside panels.
Density
Low. Centred, symmetrical, lots of cream.
Corners
Square labels; the greeting is an oval cartouche and the summary sits on rounded neck collars.
Borders
Engraved stacks of an outer burgundy line, a cream gap and an inner gilt or double line.
Shadows
A soft 12px shadow as if labels sit on bottles.
Icons
Engraved sprigs, a shield crest and ❖ dividers. Seals pair the word with a round seal for On track, a lozenge for At risk and a square seal for Behind.
Tone
Courtly and calm: The cellar, Cuvée Design, bottled by 14 November, The cellar book.
