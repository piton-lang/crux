Build

Spacing
8px unit. 16px between rows, 24px inside rows, 56px before each heading.
Density
Very low. One thing per row, one column, about five rows per screen.
Corners
14px on rows, fields and buttons. 10px on tick boxes. Round radio dots.
Borders
3px solid #222222 on every row and field; 4px on tick boxes and the danger box.
Shadows
None.
Icons
Few and large, 32 to 44px, always beside a word. Tick in a filled box means on, an empty box means off. Filled circle tick for saved, triangle for danger.
