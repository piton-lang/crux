Spacing, density and depth

8px unit. 28px housing padding, 20px between panels, 10px between transmissions.
Density
Medium. Large touch keys around a compact log.
Corners
28px housing, 16px panels and Send, 10px to 12px keys, round screws and LEDs.
Borders
2px moulded edge on the housing, 1px edges on keys, 4px left bar on each transmission.

Hard 3px to 5px bottom ledges under keys, an inset well around the screen, a deep drop under the housing.
Icons
Thick 2.4 stroke line icons. Presence as signal bars, full, two of three, or crossed out, with words.

Tone
