Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
8px unit. 2px gaps between segments and bars, 16px panel padding, 24px between panels.
Density
High. Readouts, scales and segment meters fill every panel, like test gear.
Corners
None. Every bar, segment, button and panel is square.
Borders
1px Grid lines around panels and a graticule behind the analyser. Lit borders only on focus and the pressed like control.
Shadows
None. A faint lime glow, 0 0 12px at 35 percent, sits only under the lit analyser bars.
Icons
Solid square cut transport glyphs beside a four letter caps label, such as PREV, PAUSE, NEXT. Liked uses a filled heart and the word LIKED.
Focus
A 2px lime outline offset 2px plus a lime corner tick, square.
Tone
Instrument labels in caps, such as CH A, LEVEL, UP NEXT, SRC LIBRARY.
