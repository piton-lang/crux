Spacing, density and form

Spacing
8px unit. 16px row padding, 24px column gaps, 40 to 48px between sections.
Density
Medium. Roomy rows of about 72px, a report you read top to bottom.
Corners
4px on buttons and fields. Lists and figures have no boxes at all.
Borders
A 2px ink rule opens the figures and the style sheet. 1px ink under section titles. 1px warm hairlines between rows.
Shadows
None. Structure comes from rules and whitespace.
Icons
Thin 1.6px outline icons at 18px. Status marks are solid shapes, circle, triangle, crossed square.
Focus
A 2px navy outline offset 3px. Fields get a navy border plus a 1px navy ring.
Tone
Measured and complete sentences, such as Good morning, Mara. Everything else is moving.
