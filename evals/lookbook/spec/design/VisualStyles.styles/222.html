Spacing, density and surfaces

Spacing
An 8px unit. 24px gutters and panel padding, 12px between project cards.
Density
Medium. A left Today column carries the summary so the right side can hold the chart and cards.
Corners
16px panels, 12px cards and rail items, 10px buttons and fields, pills for legend chips.
Borders
1px b200 around panels and cards, b400 on fields and the secondary button.
Shadows
None. Depth is tone, b900 rail, b100 panel, white cards on b50.
Icons
20px line icons at 1.7 to 1.8px. Status uses shape and fill weight, outlined circle check, solid triangle, inverted solid square.
Focus
2px b700 outline offset 2px plus a 6px b200 halo, white outline on the rail.
