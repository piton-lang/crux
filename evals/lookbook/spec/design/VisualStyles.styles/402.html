Space, shape and tone

Spacing
8px unit used generously. 96px above the message, 40px around the brush stroke, 64px before actions.
Density
Very sparse. One message fills the stage. The queue is six quiet lines.
Corners
None on panels. The seal has slightly uneven 3 to 7px corners, swatches are pebble shaped.
Borders
One 1px wash line between queue and stage. Secondary actions are words with a thin underline.
Shadows
None.
Icons
No icon set. Brush stroke and enso are decoration. Marks are glyph plus word. ● new, ✶ starred, ◇ enclosed. The current item has a vermilion brush bar.
Focus
1px paper outline offset 6px with a vermilion ring outside it. Fields turn their underline vermilion.
Tone
Unhurried and lowercase. one of six, seek, Next, when you are ready.
