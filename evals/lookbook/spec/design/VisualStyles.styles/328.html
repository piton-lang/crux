Corners
Square stage and barrier, 4px pass cards with a punched round hole, round play button.
Borders
A 6px truss bar with lamp circles across the top, 1px Truss rules elsewhere, tape strips instead of frames on the set list.
Shadows
None on boxes. Light does the work, a soft amber pool behind the title and beams at 12 to 18 percent.

Solid glyphs on round barrier buttons with caps words beneath. Liked is a filled heart with the word Liked. The set list marks the current song with a play arrow and NOW.
