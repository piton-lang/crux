Spacing, density and depth

Spacing
8px unit with a 14px gutter between every patch. 18px patch padding, 8px layer rows.
Density
Medium. The rail, the canvas and one column of two patches.
Corners
14px patches with a 10px stitch inset, round tools, pill buttons and fields, 6px care label.
Borders
A 1.5px dashed white running stitch 5px inside each patch. Dashed thread rules between rows. Dotted leaders in the care label.
Shadows
None on panels. Only the mobile tool bar casts a soft shadow.
Icons
18 to 20px line icons at 1.6 stroke. Selected is a circled check, Collapsed a right chevron, Saved a cloud with a check.
Focus
3px persimmon outline offset 3px.
Tone
Calm and plain, like a garment tag. Labels such as Width, Padding, Scale.
