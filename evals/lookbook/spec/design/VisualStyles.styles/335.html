Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
8px unit. 10px between lanes, 12px between capsules, 16px lane padding, 28px gutter, 16px on phones.
Density
Low to medium. One lane per stage with lots of black between.
Corners
Square lanes. Pill capsules and buttons, 18px capsules on phones.
Borders
A 2px neon left edge per lane, a fading 1px neon underline, 1px capsule outlines at 55 percent of the lane colour, dashed splits.
Shadows
No drop shadows. Glow only, a soft colour halo on figures, lane edges and the primary button.
Icons
Thin line icons at 1.9 stroke in the text colour. Stalled gets a triangle, a dashed ember outline and the word.
Focus
A 2px cyan outline offset 3px with a cyan halo.
Tone
Terse uppercase labels. Pipeline, Activity, New deal, Filter.
