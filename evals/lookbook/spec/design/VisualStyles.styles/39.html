Style sheet

Style 39 · Academic paper

The project page is typeset like a journal article, with a centred title block, an abstract, numbered sections, ruled tables, a captioned figure and footnotes.

Palette

  • Paper#fdfdfb · page
  • Ink#111111 · text, rules, primary button, figure
  • Grey#555555 · affiliations, dates, meta
  • Rule#b8b8b8 · column rule, light dividers
  • Citation red#8a1c1c · links and footnote marks

Spacing, corners and depth

Tight book leading. 8px paragraph gaps, 20px above section heads, 36px column gap with a hairline rule.

Square everywhere.
Borders

None.
