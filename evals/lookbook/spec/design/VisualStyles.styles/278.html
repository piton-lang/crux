Spacing, density and depth

Fonts
One carved serif, Iowan Old Style, Charter, Cambria, Georgia. Titles in tracked capitals, statuses in italic.
Spacing
8px unit. 24px between bays, 20px bay padding, 10px rows, 76px arch height.
Density
Medium. Three bays side by side with plain rows.
Corners
Square, except every bay rises to a pointed arch and file gems have a gabled top.
Borders
1px mortar joints around bays and between rows, a gold inner line inside each arch.
Shadows
None. Depth comes from the arches and coloured glass.
Icons
1.5px line icons inside gabled gems coloured by file type. Status is an italic word with a star, people, arrow or checked box.
Tone
Quiet and ceremonial, The files, Drive, Details.
Motifs
Pointed arches, a rose window around the preview, lancet windows for storage, faint stone coursing.
