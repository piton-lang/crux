System

Spacing
8px unit; 16px between accounts, 32 to 40px between columns, 24px section padding.
Density
Bookish and fairly dense. Two columns of accounts beside a sightings column.
Corners
3px on controls; round plates and avatars; the book rounds 14px on its fore edge.
Borders
1px ink under the running head, 1px pale rules between accounts, one boxed key.
Shadows
Stacked page edges under the spread and one soft drop. Cards have none.
Icons
Hand-drawn line pictures at 2.4 stroke on soft round washes; tiny solid dot, triangle, diamond for status.
Tone
Naturalist's notes: Field marks, Range, Look out first for, Recent sightings.
