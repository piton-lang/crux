Spacing, density and surface

Spacing
8px unit. 36px main padding, 10px between capsules, 24px inside the porthole panel.
Density
Medium. One capsule per file, about 70px tall, with a sentence of meta.
Corners
Fully round. 40px capsules, 28px panel, 22px pill buttons and fields, circle orbs and porthole.
Borders
1px #0f3a4c edges, 1.5px teal orb rims, cyan rims on folders, the selected capsule and the porthole.
Shadows
Glow only, 0 0 12px cyan at 45 percent, on lit things such as the primary button, current rail ring, selected capsule and meters.
Icons
20px line icons in foam inside round orbs. Status words pair a filled star, two people or an up arrow ring.
Focus
2px cyan outline, 3px offset, plus the cyan glow.
Tone
Quiet and plain, such as Details and 64.2 GB of 100 GB, with the sea only in the visuals.
