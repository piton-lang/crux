Spacing, corners and depth

Spacing
8px unit · 40px slab padding · 12px between rows · 48px between illustration and form
Density
Airy. Half the screen is illustration; the form holds only what the step needs.
Corners
Nearly square: 3 to 4px. Isometric faces are true parallelograms.
Borders
None on surfaces. Only planned things get a dashed 1.5px outline.
Shadows
A solid bottom face instead of blur: 10px on slabs, 5px on buttons, 4px on rows; buttons press down on click
Icons
Hexagon-based (a cube seen from the corner): outlined with tick for done, filled with play for now, dashed for planned; 3-tone cubes as avatars
Tone
Builder words, calm and plain: Built, Building, Planned
