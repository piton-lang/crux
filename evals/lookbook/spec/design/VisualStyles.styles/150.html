Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
An 8px unit. 16px between all panels, like boards in one frame, 24 to 28px inside panels.
Density
Medium. Everything sits inside one painted cupboard.
Corners
Soft. 10px panels, the crest and doors have wide arched tops, pills for buttons and the urgent banner.
Borders
A 16px blue cupboard frame, ochre inner lines inset 5px in every panel, red inner lines on doors, dotted lines between feed items.
Shadows
Only a solid darker blue ledge under the cupboard.
Icons
Painted folk shapes, C-scrolls with tulips in panel corners, small flowers on drawers. Status uses a heart for On track, a diamond with a mark for At risk and a down-pointing triangle for Behind, always with the word.
Tone
Homely and kind, such as Six doors, one for each piece of work and What the team has been up to.
