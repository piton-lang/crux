Measure and manner

Spacing
8px unit. 36 to 40px inside plans, 72 to 88px between sections. Wide margins and a centred axis.
Density
Sparse. Few words per line, generous leading, one idea per row.
Corners
None. Every edge is square.
Borders
A double frame, 1px navy then 1px gold inset 6px. Hairlines between rows. A 1px navy rule closes each table. A double gold rule under the letterhead.
Shadows
None. Depth comes from inset keylines on buttons.
Icons
No pictograms. A monogram crest, a diamond flourish, roman numerals, em dashes for list items, ✓ and — in the table, + and − on enquiries.
Focus
1px navy outline offset 4px with a 1px gold halo outside it. Fields gain a 2px gold underline.
Tone
Formal and measured, such as Schedule of fees, Particulars and Questions answered.
