System

Spacing
8px unit, generous. 24px above and below each project, 40px between sections, a 920px centred column.
Density
Low. One project per full width row, the bar is the hero of each row.
Corners
Fully round. Bars, buttons, fields, pills and the nav track are pills, the totals card 20px.
Borders
1px warm hairlines between projects and around the figure strip. No boxes around projects.
Shadows
Only the selected nav pill has a 1px soft shadow.
Icons
Rounded 1.8px line icons at 20px. Status marks are a white check in a circle for On track, a white bang in a triangle for At risk and a white bar in a square for Behind.
Tone
Warm and plain, such as Hi Mara, here is how far everything has come, and Due 7 Nov, in 7 days.
