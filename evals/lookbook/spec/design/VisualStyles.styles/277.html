Spacing, density and depth

Fonts
Marquee and stub figures in Impact, Haettenschweiler, Arial Narrow Bold. Body in Helvetica Neue, Arial.
Spacing
8px unit. 32px gutters, 10px between tickets, 24px screen padding.
Density
Medium. Tickets are single lines with a stub for size.
Corners
6px tickets with round notches cut at both ends, 4px buttons, a screen whose bottom edge curves.
Borders
1px warm edge on tickets and a 2px dashed perforation before the stub.
Shadows
A cool glow above and below the screen, a warm glow around the marquee and the selected ticket.
Icons
1.6px line icons in bulb gold. Status is a dark chip with a word and a star, people, arrow or checked box.
Tone
Showtime labels, now showing, on screen, 8 in this folder.
Motifs
A bulb dotted marquee frame, aisle floor lights in the sidebar, storage drawn as rows of seats.
