Style 238 · Night shift clinical

A calm dark teal shift board made for reading at 3 in the morning, with large legible type, figures shown as quiet readouts down a station column, projects as roomy bed style cards, a soft monitor trace for the fortnight and handover notes for activity.

Spacing, density and surfaces

Spacing
An 8px unit. 24px card padding, 20px row padding in readouts, 24px gaps, 48px minimum targets.
Density
Low. Large type and roomy cards so nothing is misread when tired.
Corners
Gentle. 12px cards, 10px buttons and fields, round bars and badges.
Borders
1px line on cards and readout dividers. The notice has a 4px muted amber left edge and the current tab a 3px teal top edge.
Shadows
None. Glare is the enemy, so depth is a small step in teal greys.
Icons
Rounded line icons at 2px stroke, 20px in tabs, sized to text elsewhere. Status uses a check circle, a triangle with a bang and a crossed square, always with the word, overdue a clock.
Focus
A 3px teal outline offset 3px, thick enough to find at a glance.
Tone
Gentle and clear, such as Hello, Mara, Handover notes and 4 tasks are overdue.
