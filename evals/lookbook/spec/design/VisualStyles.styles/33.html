Style 33 · Origami

The sign-in form is one sheet of paper folded into three panels, with crisp creases, a dog-eared corner and light triangular facets on every surface.

Spacing, corners and depth

Spacing
An 8px base. 28px by 32px panel padding, 12px between sign-in buttons, 18px between fields.
Density
Medium. One 460px sheet in three panels.
Corners
No rounding. Corners are cut on the diagonal instead, a 44px dog-ear on the sheet, 14px cuts on the primary button, small folded corners on secondary buttons.
Borders
1px crease lines with a white highlight below, 1px edge on fields and buttons. An error field gets a 3px bottom edge.
Shadows
A tight 1px drop and a soft 14px drop under the whole sheet. Facets are hard 50 percent diagonal splits, never blurred.
Icons
Everything is a triangle. A folded crane above the sheet, triangle tabs for sign-in letters, a two-tone triangle with ! for errors, ▸ on the primary button.
Tone
Neat, quiet and precise, short plain words.
