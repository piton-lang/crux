Spacing, corners and depth

Spacing
A 4px pixel grid. 16 to 20px between controls, 28px panel padding.
Density
Low. One centred panel, big touch targets, lots of sky.
Corners
Notched pixel corners, made by four flat 4px box-shadows with no blur, so each box loses one pixel at every corner.
Borders
4px ink pixel outlines on every box. Buttons add a 4px inner bevel, lighter top left and darker bottom right. Dividers are 8px dashes.
Shadows
No soft shadows. Only the hard 4px coin shadow under the brand.
Icons
Pixel art in SVG with crisp edges. A pixel sun as logo, a square ! badge on errors, letter keys on sign-in buttons, a ▶ cursor on the start button and on a focused row.
Tone
Game menu words, short and upbeat. Player 1 · ready, a 1P tag on the heading.
