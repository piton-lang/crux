Spacing, density and depth

Spacing
8px unit. 12px between stalls, 14px between tags, 20px lane padding.
Density
Relaxed. Every other tag steps in 28px so the lane meanders.
Corners
14px panels, tags with 4px left and 14px right corners, scalloped awnings, pill Send.
Borders
1px seam on panels and stalls, a 3px left edge on tags, dashed edges on reaction tickets.
Shadows
Warm glows only, around lanterns, the current stall and Send.
Icons
Paper lanterns for presence, lit, half lit or dashed unlit, each with a word. Line icons at 2 stroke elsewhere.
Focus
2px glow outline offset 3px with a soft 6px amber halo.
Tone
Warm and storybook, such as You are here, Quiet tonight, Neighbours and five notes hung today.
