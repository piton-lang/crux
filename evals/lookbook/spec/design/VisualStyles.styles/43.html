Spacing, corners and depth

Spacing
A 24px line grid set by the ruled cards. 24 to 30px between cards, 86px hanging indent after the call number.
Density
Medium. One fact per typed line, one task per card.
Corners
Square cards; 3px tab tops; 4px drawer; round punched holes.
Borders
1px warm grey card edges, 2px oak-brown drawer frame, double-line red Blocked stamp.
Shadows
A 1px edge line and a soft 2px drop under cards; an inset shadow in each punched hole.
Icons
Typed brackets [X] done, [/] in progress, [ ] planned; stamped tick for On track, slashed circle for Blocked; brass pull drawn in SVG.
Focus
2px ink outline offset 3px with a 5px buff halo.
Tone
Cataloguer’s shorthand ending in full stops: “Assigned: Aiko Tanaka.” “See also:”.
