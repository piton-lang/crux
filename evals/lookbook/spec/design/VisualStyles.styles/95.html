Style 95 · Sheet music

The dashboard engraved as a page of a printed score, with five line staves under the numbers, projects as numbered movements with tempo markings, and the fortnight's tasks as notes on a staff.

Type

One serif family, Palatino Linotype, Book Antiqua, Palatino, Georgia, serif. Markings are bold italic. Numbers in the summary use the same serif at heavy weight, stacked like a time signature.

Spacing, density and finish

Spacing
8px unit. 24px between movements, 64px between sections, wide page margins like an engraved score.
Density
Sparse. One project per line, lots of white space between staves.
Corners
Square. The only curves are note heads, ties and the fermata.
Borders
Hairline staff lines in five, a thin bar line between measures and a thin and thick double bar to close a section.
Shadows
None.
Icons
Music engraving glyphs drawn in SVG. A stemmed note for On track, a fermata for At risk, a hanging rest for Behind, each with a tempo word and the status word.
Tone
Concert programme Italian with plain English beside it, such as a tempo On track and Programme notes.
