Spacing, density and surfaces

Spacing
An 8px unit. 24px card padding, 16px gaps in grids, 32px between sections.
Density
Airy to medium. Cards breathe, six projects fill two rows of three.
Corners
Soft. 16px cards and tiles, pill buttons and search, round rings and dots.
Borders
1px line on cards and tiles, none on the sidebar. The current nav tile has a 3px moss notch.
Shadows
One soft deep shadow under cards, 0 8px 24px of near-black at 35 percent.
Icons
Rounded line icons at 1.8px stroke sized to the text. Status uses a check circle, a triangle with a bang and a crossed square, always with the word, overdue a clock.
Focus
A 2px moss outline offset 3px with a 6px dim moss halo.
Tone
Calm and kind, such as Good morning, Mara and A steady week on the trail.
