Spacing, density and depth

Fonts
Titles and file names in Palatino Linotype, Book Antiqua, Georgia, titles italic. Body in Optima, Candara, Segoe UI.
Spacing
8px unit. 40px between set list and card, 14px row padding, 32px gutters.
Density
Relaxed. Each file takes two lines, name with dotted leader to size, then meta.
Corners
Pills for buttons, search and switch. 18px velvet card with a 12px inner frame.
Borders
1px navy rules between rows. Brass hairlines frame the nav and the double frame on the card.
Shadows
A long soft drop under the velvet card and a brass glow under the primary button.
Icons
1.5px line icons in brass. Status is a coloured word with a star, people, arrow or checked box.
Tone
Warm and a little theatrical, Tonight’s files, 8 in this folder.
Motifs
