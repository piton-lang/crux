Spacing, shape and tone

Spacing
4px base, mostly 8 and 16. Rows pad 12px by 16px, reader gutter 28px.
Density
Medium. Six messages with snippets fill the list, the reader shows the whole note.
Corners
6px buttons, fields and folder items, 4px chips, round avatars.
Borders
1px #343e4b between panes and under every row. No cards.
Shadows
None. The open row has an inset 3px teal bar on its left edge.
Icons
16px line icons at 1.7 stroke. Unread is a teal dot plus bold text, starred a filled star plus the word, labels a rotated square plus the name.
Focus
2px teal outline offset 2px on every control and around the whole search field.
Tone
Plain nouns and verbs. Inbox, Reply all, 1 attachment.
