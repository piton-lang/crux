Space, shape and tone

Spacing
8px unit. 24px between the three columns, 10px cells in the ledger, 18px telegram padding.
Density
Medium. A ruled six entry book beside a full telegram.
Corners
Nearly square, 2 to 3px. Only the brass keys are pill shaped.
Borders
2px groove frames, 1px ruled cells, a 4px double rule under the sign, a brass frame with an offset outline on the telegram.
Shadows
A hard 3px dark under each key so it looks pressable. Gradients on brass.
Icons
A drawn brass key in the sign. Marks are typographic. ● New, ★ Starred, + Encl., dotted box for labels. Morse lines are decoration only.
Focus
2px bright brass outline offset 3px plus a soft brass halo.
Tone
Period office words. Received book, Pigeon rack, Time sent, STOP.
