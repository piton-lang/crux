Spacing, density and surfaces

Spacing
An 8px unit. 32px board padding, 20px between menu items, 40px between blocks.
Density
Medium. Menu items are roomy, the ticket is tight.
Corners
Mostly square with gentle 6px corners on the board, fields and buttons.
Borders
1px line around the board and chart, crema 2px rules above and below the figures, dotted leaders in line colour.
Shadows
An inset 0 0 0 4px roast band inside the board border, like a frame, and no drop shadows.
Icons
Line icons at 1.6px stroke sized to the text. Status uses a check circle, a triangle with a bang and a crossed square, always with the word, overdue a clock.
Focus
A 2px crema outline offset 3px.
Tone
Warm and unhurried, such as Good morning, Mara, The projects and Today's ticket.
