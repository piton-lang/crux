Style 174 · Icon rail master-detail

A three-pane work surface where a slim dark icon rail, a white list pane with the greeting, figures and projects, and a wide detail pane for the selected project sit side by side, quiet slate with one blue.

One system stack, system-ui, Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica Neue, Arial. Base 14px at 1.45. The detail title is 26px at 650 weight, the greeting 20px, figures 20 to 22px with tabular numbers, card titles 15px, meta 12 to 13px, and pane labels 12px caps tracked 0.06em.

System

Layout
Three columns at full height. A 64px dark slate rail holds a white square B mark, six icon-only links with tooltips and accessible names, Home current with a lighter tile and a white edge bar, and the initials disc at the foot. Next a 336px white list pane holds the greeting, the date and a pale red overdue line, then the four figures as a two by two ruled grid, then a Projects label and six full-width rows with name, status chip, category and due date, and a thin meter with percent and task counts. Mobile app 2.0 is selected with a pale blue fill and a blue left edge. The detail pane fills the rest. Its white top bar has search, New project, a bell and Mara Okafor. Below sit a breadcrumb, the project title with its At risk chip, meta with days left, an Edit project button, underlined tabs, a ruled four-cell facts strip, and then the team chart and the team activity side by side in bordered cards, activity for this project tagged. Below 1180px the cards stack. Below 760px the rail becomes a sticky top bar of icons and the panes stack.
Spacing
An 8px unit. 20px pane padding, 24px detail padding, 12px row padding, 20px between detail blocks.
Density
Medium to dense. Three panes visible at once, compact rows, small type.
Corners
Small. 6px panes and cards, 4px buttons, fields and chips, round initials.
Borders
1px light slate lines between panes, rows and fact cells. The selected row has a 3px blue left edge.
Shadows
None. Separation comes from borders and the dark rail.
Icons
Line icons at 1.75px stroke, 22px in the rail, 18px elsewhere. Status uses a check circle for On track, a triangle with a bang for At risk, a crossed square for Behind and a clock for overdue, always with the word.
Focus
A 2px blue outline offset 2px, white on the dark rail.
Tone
Plain and neutral, such as Tasks completed, all projects and Team activity.
