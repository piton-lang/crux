Print notes

Spacing
8px unit. 24px card padding, 28px gutters, 88px between sections.
Density
Medium and loose, like a zine spread. Cards tilt and stagger.
Corners
Square cards and buttons. Round only for ink blobs, halftone dots and number discs.
Borders
2px deep outline on cards with a pink duplicate offset 5px right and 4px down. Dotted 2px rules between rows. A torn zigzag strip under the header.
Shadows
No blur. Hard pink offsets of 5px by 4px behind buttons, as a second ink layer.
Texture
Fractal noise grain over the whole page at 22 percent, multiplied. Halftone dot fills on spots and blobs.
Icons
Printed glyphs only. Arrows for list items, ● for included, ✕ for not included, ○ and ● on the billing switch, a star on the sticker.
Focus
3px deep outline offset 3px with a pink offset halo. Fields gain a pink offset shadow.
Tone
Plain and a little handmade, such as Printed in two inks and plan by plan.
