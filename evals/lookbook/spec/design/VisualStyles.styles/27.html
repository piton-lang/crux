Style 27 · Aurora gradient

A modern startup page, with soft aurora mesh glows on white, a huge tightly tracked headline, a floating glassy pill nav and a generous centred hero.

Palette

  • White#ffffff · the page, windows, fields
  • Mist#f6f6fa · table heads, this sheet
  • Ink#0b0d17 · headlines, text, primary pill
  • Slate#545a6e · body copy, labels, nav links
  • Hairline#e7e8ef · borders, dividers, tracks
  • Violet#5a3ee0 · focus ring, gradient text start
  • Aurora#b8a6ff, #8de3d6, #ffc7a8, #ffb3d9 · blurred glows, icon rings; never behind small text
  • Green#157347 · On track, ringed tick

Spacing, density, corners and depth

Spacing
An 8px unit with big jumps. 72 to 112px between sections, 28 to 36px around the headline
Density
Very airy. One centred column for the hero, text capped at 520 to 640px
Corners
Pills for nav and buttons, 32px on the closing panel, 22px outer and 14px inner on the product window, 12px on fields and icons
Borders
1px #e7e8ef hairlines, 1px white edges on glass, 1.5px aurora gradient rings on icon tiles
Shadows
Long soft violet-tinted drops, 0 40px 80px −30px rgba(43,22,140,0.35) under the window, a small violet glow under the primary pill
Glass
The nav and window frame are 55 to 62% white with a 10 to 16px backdrop blur over the aurora
Icons
20px thin 1.6 stroke line icons in white tiles with a gradient ring. Status is a pill with a ringed tick, an outlined triangle or a filled square cross plus the word
Focus
A 2px violet outline offset 3px. Fields get a violet border and a 4px soft violet ring
Tone
Crisp and confident, short punchy lines, such as Less tracking. More finished work.
