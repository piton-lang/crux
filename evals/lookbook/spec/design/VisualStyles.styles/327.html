Style 327 · Club LED

The screen is a wall of LED modules in a black housing, with the title spelled in round lit dots on a matrix, a strip of colour blocks that pulse to the beat and square light pads for every control.

Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
8px unit. 4px black gaps between modules, 16px inside a module, 8px between pads.
Density
Medium high. Many small modules, each holding one job.
Corners
4px modules and pads, perfect circles for every LED dot.
Borders
1px Edge around modules. Lit pads get a 2px coloured rim and a glow.
Shadows
Only light. Lit pads and dots glow, 0 0 16px of their colour at 45 percent. No drop shadows.
Icons
Solid glyphs on pads above a caps word. The liked pad adds a filled heart and the word ON, off pads say OFF.
Focus
A 2px magenta outline offset 3px with a matching glow.
Tone
Club caps, such as ON AIR, UP NEXT, CRATES, PADS, LEVEL.
