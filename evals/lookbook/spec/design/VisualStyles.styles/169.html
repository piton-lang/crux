Style 169 · Accounting ledger

The dashboard is a page of a green bookkeeping ledger: ruled columns, black and red ink, projects kept as accounts with double-underlined totals, a tally of completed tasks and thumb-index tabs down the edge.

Palette

  • Binding#e6e2d6 · page behind the book
  • Ledger green#eef5e6 · the leaf
  • Line green#c6dcc0 · horizontal rulings, never text
  • Column green#8fb98c · money column rules
  • Head green#2f5a33 · column heads, On track tick
  • Black ink#1a1d1a · entries, figures, totals
  • Soft ink#46503f · dates, departments, notes
  • Red ink#ad1f1a · Behind, overdue
  • Umber#7a4a00 · At risk
  • Margin red#d77a73 · double margin rule, never text
  • Tab cream#f8f4e6 · thumb-index tabs
  • Focus#1f4fbf · focus ring

Spacing, density and depth

Spacing
8px unit on a 32px ruled line. Rows are 48px, sections 32px apart, an 80px left margin for the red double rule.
Density
Medium. Tabular and orderly, figures right-aligned in narrow money columns.
Corners
Square leaf. Index tabs round 8px on their outer edge, buttons 2px.
Borders
Double rule under the title, 2px green under column heads, 1px column rules, single over and double under every total.
Shadows
Two stacked 2px page edges under the leaf. Tabs cast a 2px flat edge.
Icons
Bookkeeper marks. A tick for On track, an outlined triangle for At risk, a red cross in brackets for Behind, a clock ring for Overdue. Tally marks in fives for the chart.
Tone
Counting-house language, such as Trial balance, Project accounts, Day journal and Keeper.
