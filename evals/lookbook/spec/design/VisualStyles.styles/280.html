Spacing, density and depth

Fonts
Headings and figures in Arial Black. Body in Verdana, Geneva, DejaVu Sans.
Spacing
8px unit. 28px between blocks, 14px block padding, 12px rows.
Density
Medium. Rows are plain, the blocks around them are loud.
Corners
Mixed on purpose. Square blocks and buttons, pill search, file tiles cut as squares, circles, domes and leaves.
Borders
2px white outlines on every block, tile and control, a zigzag edge on top of the details block.
Shadows
Hard offset colour blocks, 6 to 8px, lilac, teal and pink. Buttons get a 4px offset.
Icons
2.2px line icons inside filled shape tiles. Nav uses pure shapes. Status chips differ in shape, square for Starred, pill for Shared, dashed outline for Uploading.
Tone
Loud and short, Upload, New folder, Details.
Motifs
Dot grid, squiggles, confetti triangles and rings, a yellow squiggle underline and a slight tilt on the details block.
