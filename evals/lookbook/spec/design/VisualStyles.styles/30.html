Spacing, corners and depth

Spacing
An 8px base with a 28px ruled paper rhythm. 12px between sign-in buttons, 18px between fields, 36 to 40px paper padding.
Density
Medium. A two-panel folio with comfortable controls.
Corners
14px on the folio, 8px on buttons, 6px on fields and notes, 4px on paper.
Borders
Dashed 2px cream stitching inset 8px on leather. 1px tan borders on buttons and wells. A pink margin rule on the paper.
Shadows
Everything is lit from above. Inset shadows on wells and the badge, a soft drop under the paper and folio, and 1px white highlights on top edges.
Icons
Line icons debossed into leather, letter coins on sign-in buttons, a filled triangle with ! for errors, a ✓ in the checkbox.
Tone
Warm and personal, a little old-fashioned. Projects kept in one well-worn folio.
