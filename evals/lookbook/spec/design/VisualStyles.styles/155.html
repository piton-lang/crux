Style 155 · Xerox zine

A photocopied punk zine spread, stapled down the middle, with ransom-note cut-out letters, typewritten listings and nothing but toner black on white paper.

Palette

  • Paper white#ffffff
    Pages, cut-outs, fields
  • Toner#0d0d0d
    All text, bars, solid blocks, the primary button
  • Smudge#4a4a4a
    Speckle and halftone texture only
  • Wash#e6e6e6
    Grey cut-out letters and focus fill
  • Desk#c9c9c6
    The surface under the zine and tape
  • Staple#8a8f94
    Staples on the fold, decorative

Type

System

Spacing
Loose 4px base, mostly 8, 12, 18 and 32. Pages have 36px margins like a folded A5 sheet.
Density
Packed and lumpy. Listings are tight, cut-outs float with uneven gaps.
Corners
Square everywhere. Scraps are rotated rather than rounded.
Borders
Solid toner lines, 2 to 4px. Dashed lines for the fold and soft dividers.
Shadows
Only the sheet sits on the desk with a flat 6px grey offset. Speckle texture fakes copier noise.
Icons
Hand-drawn feel. Wobbly circled tick for On track, outline triangle with bang for At risk, solid black box with X for Behind, a black splat for overdue.
Tone
Blunt lower case asides and shouted caps. Scene report, track listing, deal with it today.
