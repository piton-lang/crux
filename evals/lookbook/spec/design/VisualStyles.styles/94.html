Spacing, density and finish

Spacing
8px unit. 24px gutters between stage columns, 48px between bands.

Medium. Big type up top, tighter lines lower down like a poster's fine print.
Corners
Square everywhere except round avatars and the round notification counter.
Borders
3px ink rules over each stage heading, 1px ink rules between acts and set times.
Shadows
None. Depth comes from the sunburst behind the brand.
Icons
Solid filled shapes in currentColor. Circle for On track, triangle for At risk, cross for Behind and overdue.

Billing voice in caps, such as The lineup, Fourteen nights, Tonight's warning.
