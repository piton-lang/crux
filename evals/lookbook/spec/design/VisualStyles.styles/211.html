System

Spacing
4px unit. Cells 44px tall, panel padding 10 to 14px, 16px between panels.
Density
High. Thirteen week columns by six projects in one grid, with summary rows in the footer.
Corners
4px on panels, buttons, fields and heat cells. Markers are sharp except the circle.
Borders
1px cool grey grid lines on every cell. A 2px ink rule freezes the project column, a 2px blue outline marks this week.
Shadows
None.
Icons
1.8px line icons at 16px. Status markers are a filled circle for On track, a filled diamond for At risk and a square with a white cross for Behind. A pennant flag marks the due week.
Tone
Terse and operational, such as This wk, Projects due and Activity log.
