Spacing, corners and depth

Spacing
8px unit · 48px panel padding · 16px row padding · 40px between blocks
Density
Sparse and poster-like. Big circles carry the progress.
Corners
Square blocks and chips, 3px on buttons, perfect circles for icons and avatars
Borders
None, except 2px cloud row dividers and a 4px orange underline on the row being typed
Shadows
No blur anywhere. Long shadows only: a 45° band at 20% black clipped inside each circle, and 1px-step text shadows trailing 40px from the headline
Icons
Solid white glyphs centered in flat circles, each casting a long shadow to the bottom right
Tone
Short and upbeat, status in caps: Done, Now, Next, Later
