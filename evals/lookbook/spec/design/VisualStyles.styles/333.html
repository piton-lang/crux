Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
8px unit. The lyric stage is at least 60 percent of the first screen, with 48px around the lines and 12px inside pills.
Density
Very low in the middle, compact at the edges.
Corners
Fully rounded pills for the strip, the controls and buttons, 20px ticket cards.
Borders
2px Booth line rims on pills and tickets. The sung part of the line carries a 6px rounded yellow bar under it.
Shadows
A soft yellow glow, 0 0 24px at 40 percent, behind the sung words only.
Icons
Chunky solid glyphs in round buttons. A yellow ball marks the next word. Liked is a filled pink heart with the word Liked. Toggles say Off.
Focus
A 3px yellow outline offset 3px, following the pill shape.
Tone
Friendly and loud, such as Sing along, Up next to sing, Your songbook and Next.
