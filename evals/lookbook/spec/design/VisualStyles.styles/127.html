System

Spacing
6px gutters between tiles like a printed chart; 8px tile padding; 28px between the table and each series row.
Density
Dense grid of equal cells. Eight columns wide, four below 1000px.
Corners
Square on every tile, field and button.
Borders
1.5px ink around every cell; dashed for the key; 3px rule above series labels.
Shadows
None. Colour families and borders do all the grouping.
Icons
Tiny solid glyphs at 14px: circle, triangle and heavy cross. Symbols replace icons elsewhere.
Tone
Chemistry-class labels: Key, Reactions, the periodic table of your week. Plain values otherwise.
