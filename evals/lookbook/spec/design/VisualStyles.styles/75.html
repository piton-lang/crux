Spacing, corners and depth

Spacing
8px unit. 12 to 20px between tiles and panels, 14 to 18px inside.
Density
Low to medium. Big readings, compact town cards on the map.
Corners
Round and soft. 18px panels, 14 to 16px tiles and town cards, pills for nav and buttons.
Borders
Almost none. A blue header bar on each panel and an 8px red edge on the warning.
Shadows
Soft 0 6px 18px navy at 8 percent under panels, a little stronger under town cards.
Icons
Forecast glyphs. Sun with rays for On track, cloud over sun for At risk, dark cloud with rain for Behind, a red triangle with a bolt for overdue.
Tone
A friendly presenter: Storm warning, conditions now, sunny, clouding over, heavy rain.
