Spacing, density and depth

Spacing
8px unit. 28px canvas padding, 18px panel padding, 8px between wells.
Density
Medium. Big 50px chrome tools, compact layer and property rows.
Corners
Round everywhere. Circles for tools and handles, 17 to 19px pills for buttons and wells, 14px trays.
Borders
1px dark edges under a 1px white top highlight. Panels part with a black line over a faint light line.
Shadows
Inset shadows sink wells and trays. Chrome pills sit proud with a 2px drop. Share has a double ring bezel.
Icons
18px line icons at 1.9 stroke. Selected is a circled check, Collapsed a right chevron, Saved a cloud with a check.
Focus
2px ice blue outline offset 3px with a 12px ice glow.
Tone
Precise and technical. Caps labels such as Position, Size, Export.
