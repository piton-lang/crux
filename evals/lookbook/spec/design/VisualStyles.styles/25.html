Spacing, density, corners and depth

Spacing
An 8px unit, but placement is deliberately uneven. Indents of 48px, staggered card offsets of 24 and 72px
Density
Loose and busy at once. Few words, lots of shapes in the margins
Corners
Mixed on purpose. Square blocks, full circles, and single 26 to 64px rounded corners on one side only
Borders
3px solid ink on every block, 2px dashed ink between list rows, zigzag edges on the logo band
Shadows
No blur. Each block sits on an offset patterned twin, diagonal stripes, a dot grid or a solid colour, 14 to 17px down and right
Pattern
Squiggle lines, zigzags, rings, crosses and tilted triangles scattered as confetti; small tilts of 2 to 12 degrees
Icons
Large 64px filled geometric glyphs with 3px outlines. Status uses a circle for On track, a triangle for At risk and a cross for Overdue, each with the word on a differently shaped chip
Focus
A 3px dashed cobalt outline offset 4px, yellow on cobalt. Fields also get a mint offset block
Tone
Cheerful and chatty, such as Hey, small teams! and Three things it does really well
