Style 274 · Rainy neon city

A wet night street where the file list glows like shop signs on rain soaked asphalt, pink and cyan neon tubes, soft bokeh and reflections pooling under every row.

Spacing, density and depth

Fonts
Condensed sans stack. Avenir Next Condensed, Roboto Condensed, Arial Narrow, then system-ui, with font-stretch condensed.
Spacing
8px unit. 32px page gutters, 24px between columns, 12px row padding, 20px window padding.
Density
Medium. Eight rows fit with room for the reflection lines.
Corners
2px everywhere, so neon reads as glass tubes and signs.
Borders
1px dark steel lines. Rows fade into a centred reflection line, the selected row gets a pink to cyan line.
Shadows
Coloured glows only, pink around the details window and primary button, cyan on focus.
Icons
Line icons at 1.6px, 22px in rows, cyan for files and amber for folders. Status is an outlined tube with icon and word.
Tone
Short caps labels, Upload, New folder, Details.
Motifs
Blurred bokeh discs and fine diagonal rain over the header, clipped by its own section.
