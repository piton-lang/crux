Surface rules

Spacing
8px unit. Form inset 30px inside a double printed frame, 9px row padding, 28px before sections.
Density
Dense like a form. Data in columns of printed boxes, one wire per project.
Corners
Square everywhere. No rounding at all.
Borders
1px form print rules, a 3px double rule under the masthead, a 2px plus 1px frame round the form.
Shadows
Only the tape strips lift, with a 1px shadow like glued paper, and the form has a soft drop.
Icons
Few. A lightning bolt seal. Status codes pair a word with a filled dot, triangle or square; overdue is red text. Nav uses printed checkboxes with an X.
Tone
Clipped wire language in caps, ending phrases with STOP, such as Urgent 4 tasks are overdue STOP.
Focus
3px form print outline offset 2px over a pale yellow fill.
