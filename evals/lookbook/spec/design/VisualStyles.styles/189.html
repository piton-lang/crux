System

Type stack
Seravek, Gill Sans Nova, Ubuntu, Calibri, Optima, Candara, DejaVu Sans, sans-serif. One family, weights 400 and 600.
Layout
Cream top bar with brand, text nav and actions. A centred 1120px column: greeting beside a four-figure strip, then projects at left and chart over activity at right.
Spacing
8px unit. 24px panel padding, 32px between sections, 16px row padding.
Density
Comfortable. Six rows and six activity items fit without crowding.
Corners
6px on panels, buttons, fields and chips. Avatars are round.
Borders
1px sand lines between rows and around panels.
Shadows
One soft warm shadow, 0 1px 2px rgba(80,50,30,.06), on panels only.
Icons
Thin 1.6px line icons at 16 to 18px in bark. Status symbols are line shapes in the status colour.
Focus
A 2px terracotta outline offset 2px. Fields add a clay fill.
Tone
Warm and plain, in sentence case. Welcome back, Mara. 4 tasks are overdue.
