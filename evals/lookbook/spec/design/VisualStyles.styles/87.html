Spacing, shape and voice

Spacing
8px unit. Generous 56px sheet margins, 40px between letter parts, 20px inside pigeonholes.
Density
Low to medium. A letter, a folio of four lines and six fobs.
Corners
Square paper and rack. Key fobs are rounded at the top to 120px and 28px at the foot.
Borders
1px navy rules with a 4px inset linen line on the folio. Thin brass plates on each pigeonhole.
Shadows
The sheet floats on a soft 32px shadow. Fobs hang with a 6px shadow. The crest uses a one pixel light and dark emboss.
Icons
Fine 1.5px line icons, a desk bell for notifications. On track is a diamond, At risk a triangle, Behind a circle with a bar, overdue a clock.
Tone
Courteous and formal. Kindly note, We would suggest, With our compliments, Departure 14 Nov.
