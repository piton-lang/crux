Spacing, density, corners and depth

Spacing
4px base. 12px between floating panels, 20 to 28px between frames, 26px control height.
Density
High in the panels, medium inside the frames.
Corners
10px floating panels, 6px buttons and fields, 5px rows, 4px frames.
Borders
Almost none. Fields are filled wells. A 1.5px blue outline with square handles marks a selection.
Shadows
Panels float on a 1px 6 percent ring plus 4px 12px and 12px 32px soft shadows. Frames get a single 1px 2px shadow.
Icons
14px line icons at 1.3 stroke. A tiny hash grid marks each frame. Status uses 8px solid circle, triangle and square with the word.
Tone
Tool-like nouns and short labels. Pages, Sections, Selected in Projects, Days left.
