Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
4px bezels between every screen. 18 to 20px tile padding. No outer margin beyond one bezel.
Density
Low in items, high in size. Every tile holds one idea at wall scale.
Corners
None anywhere, like display panels.
Borders
A 10px status band across the top of each tile, green, amber, blue or red. 2px rules inside lists.
Shadows
None. Black bezels separate screens.
Icons
Filled status shapes at 44px beside 34px status words, circle Healthy, triangle Degraded, diamond Recovered, octagon Firing, square Resolved, magnifier Investigating. Chart lines 5 to 6px.
Focus
An inset 4px white ring plus an 8px signal blue inner ring, drawn inside tiles so bezels never hide it.
