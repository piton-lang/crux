Spacing, density, corners and depth

Spacing
An 8px unit, generous 96px sections, 32px between cards and 56px between hero columns
Density
Relaxed brochure pace. Large type, short lines, plenty of sky
Corners
Soft everywhere. Arched card tops of 160px, 20 to 28px card corners, 18px fields, a leaf-shaped button with one tight 8px corner
Borders
Almost none. A 2px dashed inner line on the postcard, 2px sand rules between rows, a dotted stamp edge
Shadows
Warm teal drops, 0 24px 40px −28px rgba(6,78,87,0.5), and a solid darker coral lip under the primary button
Shapes
Wavy section edges, palm-leaf silhouettes at the corners, a sun disc, luggage tags on a line for logos, a postcard with a stamp
Icons
Small filled scene illustrations in palette colours inside arched feature pictures. Status uses a filled circle tick, a filled triangle with ! and a rounded square with ×, beside the word
Focus
A 3px coral outline offset 3px, sun yellow on deep water. Fields add a sea border and a soft sea glow
Tone
Warm and unhurried holiday language, such as Greetings from this week and Everything your team needs to unwind
