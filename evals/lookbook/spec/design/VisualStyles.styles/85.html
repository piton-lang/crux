Spacing, shape and voice

Spacing
8px unit. 24px between packets, 32px between panels, 6px inner frame gap.
Density
Medium. Each packet carries a picture, a meter and two facts.
Corners
Square, 2px at most. The masthead has a pinked edge.
Borders
3px team colour frame plus a 1px inner frame on packets. 2px ink on panels with an inset hairline.
Shadows
A flat 4px offset tint behind packets, like card stock on a shelf.
Icons
Small painted plant vignettes per team. Status uses a check in a circle for On track, a mark in a diamond for At risk and a cross in a square for Behind. Overdue uses the diamond.
Tone
Gardener's notes. Germination, Sprouted, Harvest, Sow these first.
