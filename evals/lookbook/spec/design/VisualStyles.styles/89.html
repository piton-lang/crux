8px unit. 72px outer and 88px inner page margins, 48px under running heads, 36px between plates.

Corners
Square throughout.
Borders
1px hairlines under running heads and between entries, a 1px ink frame inside each plate.
Shadows
Each spread lifts off the table with a soft 40px shadow and a shaded gutter down its middle.
Icons
Small solid shapes. On track a filled circle, At risk a filled triangle, Behind a flat bar and overdue a diamond, always with the caps word.
