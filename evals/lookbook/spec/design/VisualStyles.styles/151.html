Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
An 8px unit. 24px between panels, 12 by 20px rows, tiles on a 10 to 12px grid with 1.6px grout.
Density
Medium to dense. Many small tiles, calm text around them.
Corners
Nearly square. Tiles 1.2px, controls 3px, the avatar is a round tile cluster.
Borders
Panels have a 2px basalt line, a 6px grout band and an inner basalt line. Tessera bands of basalt and ochre wave run under the header and feed.
Shadows
None. Depth comes from grout and slightly rotated tiles.
Icons
Simple line icons for tools and tessera shapes for markers. Status uses a round tile for On track, a triangular tile for At risk and a square tile with a hole for Behind, always with the word.
Tone
Plain and craft minded, such as one tile per 5% and One row of tiles per task.
