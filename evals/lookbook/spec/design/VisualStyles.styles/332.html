Style 332 · Cassette deck

The player is a front loading cassette deck, with a tape window of turning reels, a mechanical counter, two amber VU meters with needles, a row of chrome piano keys for transport and the queue typed on the cassette's insert card.

Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
8px unit. 24px deck padding, 16px between modules, keys touch with 2px seams.
Density
Medium. A full machine face, with the queue on a separate card.
Corners
6px deck and meter bezels, 2px piano keys, 12px tape window.
Borders
1px Bezel rings around windows, with a dark inner line. Ribs run across the body as 2px stripes.
Shadows
Raised chrome keys cast 0 4px 0 #000. The pressed key drops to 0 1px 0 and sits 3px lower. The window has an inset shadow.
Icons
Classic transport glyphs printed on the key with the word underneath. The pressed Play key also shows a lit amber lamp and the word Playing. Toggles say On or Off.
Focus
A 2px amber outline offset 3px on every key, window and card row.
Tone
Hardware legends, such as Tape counter, Insert, Tape shelf, Lyric memo, Play, Pause.
