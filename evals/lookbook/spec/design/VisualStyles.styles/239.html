Style 239 · Chalkboard

The whole dashboard is written up on a slate chalkboard in a wooden frame, with handwritten headings, wobbly chalk boxes, tally marks beside the counts, hatched chalk bars and soft eraser smudges.

Spacing, density and surfaces

Spacing
An 8px unit. 20px box padding, 24px gaps, 40px between board sections.
Density
Medium. Boxes are generous like a teacher's board.
Corners
Hand drawn. Every box uses uneven elliptical corners so no two edges match.
Borders
2px chalk lines with uneven corners, dashed for the search box, a 14px wooden frame round the board.
Shadows
No drop shadows. Text carries a 1px chalk blur and the board carries soft white smudges at 4 to 6 percent.
Icons
Chalk line icons at 2.2px stroke with rough round ends. Status uses a drawn circle with a tick, a triangle with a bang and a crossed box, always with the word, overdue a clock.
Focus
A 3px dashed yellow outline offset 4px, like a ring drawn in chalk.
Tone
Friendly classroom, such as Good morning, Mara!, Still to do and What happened today.
