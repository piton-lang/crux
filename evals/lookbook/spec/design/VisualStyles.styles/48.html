Feel

Spacing
8px unit, generous. 32px card padding and gaps, 56px above the page title, 18px row padding.
Density
Airy. Few rows per screen, lots of oat around them.
Corners
18px cards, 12px fields, fully round buttons, chips and switches.
Borders
Thin 1px warm hairlines only; the current tab gets a 2px sage underline.
Shadows
None. Depth comes from oat versus linen.
Icons
Thin 1.4 stroke round icons at text size. A check in the switch knob means on, a dash means off. Selected chips show a check.
