Form and finish

Spacing
8px unit. 10px between lanes, 18 to 24px inside panels, 32px between areas.
Density
Medium. One lane per day, each about 104px tall, with times placed on a 09:00 to 17:00 dial.
Corners
Pills for controls. Lanes use alternating 18px and 60px corners like a boomerang. Event bars use 12px and 2px corners.
Borders
2px walnut on secondary buttons and fields, dotted 2px tan dividers in the console.
Shadows
Hard offsets only. A 4px mustard offset under the primary button, a walnut ledge under the console.
Icons
Solid filled shapes at text size. Dot for Team, four point starburst for Deadline, boomerang for Out of office.
Focus
3px mustard outline with a 6px walnut halo.
Tone
Breezy and period: The week ahead, Calendars on air, Nothing on the dial.
