Spacing, density and depth

Spacing
8px unit used generously. 32px between floating cards, 20px card padding, 10px layer rows.
Density
Airy. Few lines per card, wide gutters of marble showing between them.
Corners
2px stone cards, round handles and avatars, full pill buttons.
Borders
One 1px gold edge at 55 percent on each card. Hairline rules inside. Fields are underlines only.
Shadows
Deep and soft, 0 30px 60px black at 70 percent under cards and 0 40px 80px under the frame.
Icons
17px fine line icons at 1.2 stroke in gold. Selected is a circled check, Collapsed a right chevron, Saved a cloud with a check.
Focus
1px gold outline offset 3px with a 4px gold glow ring.
Tone
Reserved and elegant. Didone titles with spaced caps labels such as Position, Export.
