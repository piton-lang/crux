Spacing, density and surfaces

Spacing
An 8px unit. 28px panel padding, 20px between glasses, 32px between panels.
Density
Medium. A rich shelf with room around each glass.
Corners
Small. 4px panels, plaques, buttons and fields, glasses with rounded bases.
Borders
Brass. A 3px brass rail under the header, double bevelled brass frames on plaques, 1px line on panels and ledger rules.
Shadows
A warm amber glow behind the greeting and this week bars, and inner shadows that make plaques look inset.
Icons
Line icons at 1.7px stroke in brass. Status uses a check circle, a triangle with a bang and a crossed square, always with the word, overdue a clock.
Focus
A 2px brass outline offset 3px with a faint amber glow.
Tone
Warm and a little formal, such as Good morning, Mara, The shelf and The ledger.
