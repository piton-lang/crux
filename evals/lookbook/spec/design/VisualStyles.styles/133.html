Style 133 · Warehouse logistics

The dashboard as a warehouse dispatch board, with aisle signs for navigation, numbers on hazard-striped counters, each project a shipping label with a bin location, a barcode and cartons loaded, and the daily chart as a row of conveyor belts.

Palette

  • Concrete#e9e8e3 · floor, with a faint 120px grid
  • Label white#ffffff · labels, panels, fields
  • Black#111111 · text, 2 to 3px borders, aisle signs, barcodes
  • Steel#4f4f4b · small codes, meta
  • Forklift yellow#f5c400 · top band, bins, current aisle, focus halo, never text
  • Kraft#c89b62 · cartons, label offset shadow, edged #8a6233
  • Rule#b9b8b0 · table rules
  • On track#17663a · ticked box
  • At risk#8a4b00 · triangle
  • Behind#b3121b · crossed octagon, overdue tag, late label shadow

Type

Labels and text: "Arial Narrow", "Roboto Condensed", "Helvetica Neue Condensed", Arial, sans-serif, bold caps for labels. Codes and numbers: "OCR A Std", "OCR A Extended", ui-monospace, Menlo, Consolas, monospace.
