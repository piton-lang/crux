Spacing, density and depth

Fonts
Headings, labels and figures in a geometric sans, Futura, Century Gothic, Avenir Next, Josefin Sans. Body in Avenir Next, Gill Sans, Segoe UI.
Spacing
8px unit. 32px between frames, 28px frame padding, 12px cells.
Density
Medium. A three column register, name, modified and size.
Corners
Square frames with a stepped gold square at each corner. No rounding anywhere.
Borders
3px double gold around frames, double rules under table heads and the nav, 1px dark rules between rows.
Shadows
None. Depth comes from double rules and inset gold rings on the primary button.
Icons
1.5px line icons inside gold diamonds. Status is a spaced caps word with a star, people, arrow or checked box.
Tone
Formal and spare, Register, Details, 8 entries.
Motifs
A sunburst fan behind the title, a diamond rule, fan rays behind the preview and a fan shaped storage gauge.
