Style 145 · Roman inscription

The dashboard is a marble temple front, with the brand carved in a pediment, the four totals on fluted columns and each project cut into an inscribed tablet with dovetail handles.

Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
An 8px unit. 24px between columns, 28 by 60px between tablets to leave room for their handles, 48px between bands.
Density
Sparse and monumental. Everything is centred on one axis.
Corners
Square stone. Tablets carry dovetail handles on both sides, column capitals carry round volutes.
Borders
Mouldings as inner frames, a 1px groove line, an 8px gap, then another groove. Double rules under the architrave.
Shadows
Inset shadows for carved text, grooves and fields, and a soft cast shadow under column bases.
Icons
Plain line icons for tools. Status uses Roman interpuncts, a round dot for On track, a triangle for At risk and a square for Behind, beside the caps word.
Tone
Inscriptional and brief in spaced capitals, such as Salve, Mara and Acta diurna, with plain English for the facts.
