Style 173 · Soft UI

The dashboard is moulded from one pale grey surface: panels, knobs and buttons are extruded by paired light and dark shadows, wells and grooves are pressed in, yet text keeps full AA contrast and focus is a solid indigo ring.

Spacing, density and depth

Spacing
8px unit. 16 to 20px between raised items so shadows can breathe, 28px between sections.
Density
Low to medium. Few, large soft objects.
Corners
Very round. 20 to 28px panels, 14px buttons and nav, full circles for knobs and icon buttons, pills for fields and chips.
Borders
None. Edges come only from shadow pairs.
Shadows
Raised uses 7px 7px 14px lowlight with minus 7px highlight. Small raised is 4px. Pressed wells use the same pair inset. Current nav is pressed in.
Icons
Line icons at 1.7px for nav. Status icons are solid, a check circle for On track, a triangle for At risk, a rounded crossed square for Behind, a clock ring for Overdue, inside pressed chips with their word.
Tone
Plain and neutral, such as Projects, 6 active and Activity.
