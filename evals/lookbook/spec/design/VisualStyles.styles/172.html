Style 172 · Kawaii

The dashboard is a sheet of cute stickers: pastel blobs with little faces hold the totals, every corner is round, sparkles float behind, and each status wears its own face on its own shape.

Spacing, density and depth

Spacing
8px unit. 18px gaps between stickers, 28 to 36px between sections.
Density
Airy and playful. Big soft shapes, one idea per sticker.
Corners
Round everything. 26 to 28px cards, full pills for buttons, fields, chips and bars, wobbly blob radii on the totals.
Borders
A 2.5 to 3px plum outline on every shape, like a die-cut sticker.
Shadows
Hard plum drop of 4 to 6px straight down, no blur.
Icons
Faces on shapes. A smiling circle for On track, a wobbly-mouthed rounded triangle for At risk, a sad rounded square for Behind, a star with a bang for Overdue. Totals have their own moods.
Tone
Cheerful and kind, such as Good morning, Mara!, Uh-oh and What’s new, while facts stay exact.
