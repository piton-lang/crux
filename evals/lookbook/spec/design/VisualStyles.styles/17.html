System

Spacing
8px unit used generously. 96px between sections, 36px between portfolio rows, 40px inside figure cells.
Density
Very sparse. One centred column of 880px, one to two elements per row.
Corners
Square throughout. Only palette swatches are round.
Borders
1px hairline rules in #d9d0bd between entries and sections. A 1px ink rule opens the style sheet. A 40px gold rule under the wordmark.
Shadows
None.
Icons
No pictograms. Status is a 12px thin outline mark, a circle, a lozenge or a struck square, beside a tracked capital word. Notices are a word with a count.
Tone
Courteous and composed, with titled sections such as The Week, The Portfolio, The Fortnight and Correspondence, and small numbers written out in prose.
