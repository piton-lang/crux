Overdue tasks by project

Open tasks whose due_on is before today, counted per project, biggest first.

sqlran at 09:41
SELECT p.name AS project, count(*) AS open_overdue
FROM tasks t
JOIN projects p ON p.id = t.project_id
WHERE t.status <> 'done'
  AND t.due_on < current_date
GROUP BY p.name
ORDER BY open_overdue DESC;
