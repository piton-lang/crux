Spacing, density and finish

Spacing
8px unit. 24px between cards, 40px between sections, generous inner margins inside every frame.
Density
Calm and sparse. Six cards in one row on a wide screen, little text on each.
Corners
Arched tops on cards and altar windows, a full round arch over a 6px base. Buttons are pills.
Borders
A 1px gold leaf frame with a second hairline 5px inside, like a printed card border.
Shadows
One soft violet lift, 0 6px 20px rgba(42,35,80,0.10), on cards and panels.
Icons
Fine 1.2px gold line drawings, a star, a wheel, a lantern, scales, a blossom and a winged letter. Status marks are solid, a sun for On track, a crescent for At risk, a lightning bolt for Behind and overdue.
Tone
Soft and a little ceremonial but plain, such as The reading, The spread and Recent signs.
