Style 111 · Sticker sheet

The dashboard is a sheet of glossy die-cut stickers with thick white borders on a pale backing, each number, project and chart bar its own sticker, slightly tilted as if just placed.

System

Spacing
An 8px unit with wide 24 to 32px gutters so each sticker has room for its white border.
Density
Low to medium. Big type, three stickers across.
Corners
Round. 20 to 28px on stickers, full pills for nav, buttons and chart bars, one circle sticker.
Borders
A 5 to 6px white die-cut border drawn as a spread shadow. Dashed kiss-cut lines on the backing.
Shadows
A soft 8px lift under every sticker, plus a diagonal gloss highlight across the top left.
Icons
Filled sticker shapes. Ticked circle for On track, rounded triangle for At risk, rounded square with a bar for Behind, red starburst for overdue.
Tone
Bubbly, such as Hey Mara and Just stuck on.
