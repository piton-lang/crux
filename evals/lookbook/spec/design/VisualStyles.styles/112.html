Style 112 · Polaroid wall

The dashboard is a wall of instant photos taped up with washi tape, where captions are written by hand in the white margins and each project photo develops from left to right as its work is done.

System

Spacing
An 8px unit. Frames pad 12px with a deep caption margin, 32 to 48px of wall between photos.
Density
Low. Few large photos, generous wall.
Corners
Square photos and frames. Controls 4px.
Borders
None. The white frame is the border.
Shadows
A soft 18px drop under each photo, tilted 1 to 3 degrees either way.
Icons
Hand-drawn marker outlines. Ticked circle for On track, triangle for At risk, square with a dash for Behind, star for overdue.
Tone
Casual notes on the back of a photo, such as Morning, Mara and Who did what.
