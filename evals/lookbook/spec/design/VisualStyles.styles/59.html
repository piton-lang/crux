Form and finish

Spacing
8px unit. 24px between sheets, 16 to 20px inside, 10px between labels.
Density
Airy. Seven sheets in a flowing grid, never more than two labels per sheet.
Corners
Square everywhere. Only the today stamp and avatar are round.
Borders
1px ink on labels and buttons. Deadlines use a 3px double rust rule, out of office a dashed slate rule.
Shadows
One flat paper offset, 2px 3px 0 sepia at 12 percent. Two strips of translucent tape on each sheet.
Icons
Fine 1.4 stroke ink line art at text size. Leaf for Team, pennant for Deadline, crossed square for Out of office. Large pencil sprigs decorate each sheet.
Focus
2px dashed ink outline offset 3px. Fields gain an olive wash.
Tone
Calm and catalogue-like: Collected this week, Nothing scheduled, Sheet 04.
