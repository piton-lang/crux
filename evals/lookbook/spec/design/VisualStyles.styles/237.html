Spacing, density and surfaces

Spacing
A 4px unit. 12px panel padding, 8px by 12px grid cells, 16px between blocks.
Density
Dense. Many rows and small type, everything visible at once on a laptop.
Corners
Square everywhere, 0px on tiles, panels, buttons and fields.
Borders
1px line around every pane, panel and grid row. A 3px blue edge marks the current tree item and a 4px coral edge marks the message bar.
Shadows
None. Depth comes from four flat greys stepping from base to header.
Icons
Fine line icons at 1.5px stroke, 16px. Status uses a check circle, a triangle with a bang and a crossed square, always with the word, overdue a clock.
Focus
A 2px blue outline inset by 1px inside grid rows and offset 2px elsewhere.
Tone
Formal and exact, such as Overview, Projects, 6 items and Activity, last 24 hours.
