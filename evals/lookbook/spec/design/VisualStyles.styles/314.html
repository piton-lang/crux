Style 314 · Gaming RGB

A gamer HUD for the service monitor, with chamfered black panels framed by a cyan to magenta to lime gradient edge, services as a roster of cards with uptime health bars, and terse caps labels.

Spacing, density, corners, borders, shadows, icons, tone

Spacing
8px unit. 24px page gutter, 16px between panels, 16px panel padding, 10px between roster cards.
Density
Dense HUD. Roster, three charts and the incident column visible at once on a laptop.
Corners
No radius. Panels have 14px chamfers at top left and bottom right, cards a 10px chamfer at top right, the logo is a hexagon.
Borders
Every panel has a 2px RGB gradient edge. Cards carry a 3px left status edge. 1px line dividers.
Shadows
None for depth. Only the request line glows with a 4px cyan drop shadow.
Icons
Line icons at 1.8 stroke for navigation. Filled status shapes, circle Healthy, triangle Degraded, diamond Recovered, octagon Firing, square Resolved, magnifier Investigating, always with the word.
Focus
A 2px white outline offset 3px plus a 5px magenta glow ring.
Tone
Terse uppercase HUD labels such as Service roster, Alert feed, Patch notes and PEAK 9,400 · 14:00.
