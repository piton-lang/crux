System

Spacing
An 8px unit. Labels pad 16 to 22px, 16px between labels on the rail.
Density
Medium. Six tall narrow labels in one row on wide screens.
Corners
Square cut fabric. The swing tag has clipped corners and a punched hole, hanger loops are round.
Borders
1.5px gold or cream frames around numbers, dashed seam lines where strips are sewn on.
Shadows
None, except a 1px lift on the satin label.
Icons
Laundry care style outlines. A square with a filled dot for On track, an outlined triangle for At risk, a filled tub with a down arrow for Behind, an iron for overdue.
Tone
Clothing label words, such as On the rail, Care note and Care and content.
