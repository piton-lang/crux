System

Layout
Three full-height columns of 232px, the flexible centre and 344px. The white left sidebar holds a green rounded square mark and the wordmark, a Workspace caption over six icon and label nav items with Home filled pale green and a count of 6 beside Projects, and a Needs attention group linking Billing migration and Mobile app 2.0 with their status. The centre has a white top bar with a wide search field and a green New project button, then the greeting, date and red overdue line, then the four figures as rounded pill chips in one row, overdue in pale red with a clock. Under a Projects heading the feed is six separate white cards, each with a 44px green progress ring holding the percent, name over category and task count, due date over days left, and the status at the right end. The pale green right panel starts with a bell and Mara Okafor, then a Tasks completed caption, two small white tiles with last week 59 and this week 73 up 14, the fourteen-day bar chart in a white well with outlined last week bars and green this week bars, and an Activity timeline down a vertical line with hollow green nodes. Below 1200px the right panel drops under the feed as two columns. Below 760px the nav becomes a three by two grid, the bell and user sit top right, and everything stacks.
Spacing
An 8px unit. 32px centre gutters, 20px panel padding, 8px between feed cards, 12 to 16px card padding.
Density
Medium. Three columns of context at once, roomy cards in the centre.
Corners
Rounded. 8px cards, buttons, fields and tiles, 12px on the style sheet, pill chips, round rings and nodes.
Borders
1px cool grey borders on cards, chips and sidebars. The right panel uses pale green edged tiles.
Shadows
None. Columns separate by tone, white left, cool white centre, pale green right.
Icons
Line icons at 1.8px stroke, 18px, muted in the nav and green when current. Status uses a check circle for On track, a triangle for At risk, a crossed square for Behind and a clock for overdue, always with the word.
Focus
A 2px green outline offset 2px on every control.
Tone
Friendly and plain, with lowercase chip labels such as tasks due this week and short captions such as Needs attention.
