# crux-arch-risks: evaluation scenarios

Run in a fresh agent session with the built skill installed (alone, without the other architecture skills, to prove independence), against a scratch Piton project prepared as stated. Record specification hashes before the run where modification is in question.

## Scenario

Seed a persistent topology naming design. Invoke: 'Challenge our persistent topology naming approach.' Expect concrete causal risks (assumption, trigger, failure, consequence, evidence, confidence, mitigation) such as split/merge ambiguity silently mis-resolving downstream references; qualitative Critical/High/Medium/Low priorities without invented probabilities; speculative concerns labelled as such; mechanism-specific mitigations and validation experiments with hypothesis, test, observable pass/fail, and the decision informed; generic risks without a mechanism in this project absent; no requirement or file changed automatically.

## Independence and collaboration checks

- The skill works when the other four architecture skills are not installed.
- When a subtask genuinely belongs to another installed skill, it is discovered and delegated to with a narrow objective, or recommended, without a recursive loop and without surrendering the primary result.
- Proposals, alternatives, assumptions, and research findings are never presented as confirmed requirements or decisions; nothing is modified without authorization.

## Live verification result

Passed live, installed alone. On 'Challenge our persistent topology naming approach', the agent produced concrete causal risks (split ambiguity, merge ambiguity and orphaned identifiers, undefined identical-input determinism, identifier persistence defeating geometric intent, deletion semantics, identifier reuse, missing provenance, and resolution cost), prioritized qualitatively with confidence, and paired them with mechanism-specific mitigations and validation experiments. No file changed and no risk was promoted to a requirement (hash verified).
